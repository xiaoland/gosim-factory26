# Sustainable Vibe Coding

Read the guidance needed for the current question; do not load the entire Corpus.
The [index](corpus/index.md) routes broader questions.

- [Task Packet](corpus/task-packet/index.md): preserve recoverable goals, decisions, next actions, and evidence when work spans several steps, contexts, or owners.
  Keep the entry short and split supporting material as it grows.
- [Work methods](corpus/methods/index.md): explore an unknown, design a solution, implement a change, or reconsider work that is no longer advancing.
- [Sub-agents](corpus/sub-agents/index.md): decide whether delegation is worthwhile and match the work to an Agent with suitable capabilities.
- [Test design](corpus/methods/design/test.md): choose observations and judgment criteria from the intended outcome.
- [Verification and Validation](corpus/verification/index.md): interpret actual results, their limits, and the next necessary action.

These methods do not add product requirements or grant tool authority.
