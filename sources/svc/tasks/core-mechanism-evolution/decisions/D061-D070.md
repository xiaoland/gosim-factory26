# Decisions D-061..D-070

## D-061 — Model Is Embedded Working-Method Logic, Not Another Foundational Method

- **State**: accepted for design; durable corpus landing and real-task effect
  pending
- **Authority and date**: Sir, 2026-08-08
- **Accepts and refines**: `V-144..V-145` and
  [`design/51`](../design/51-model-specialist-method.md)
- **Decision**:
  - Do not promote `Model` into another separately selected top-level
    foundational Working Method.
  - Treat task-fit representation, evidence grounding, consequential
    compression, and intended-use challenge as embedded modeling logic. A
    Working Method uses only the parts its own return requires; the logic is
    not a universal four-step ritual or mandatory artifact.
  - Explore may retain `Model` as an Agent-oriented epistemic Route/job handle
    with progressive specialist guidance. It does not become required Human
    common ground or introduce separate runtime state.
  - Keep simple models implicit. Externalize the smallest useful part only
    under real consumer, complexity, persistence, delegation, or challenge
    pressure; do not create a default `model.md`, diagram, or durable truth.
  - Method-local use/challenge qualifies whether the representation supports
    its immediate purpose; consequential independent claim qualification
    remains with Verification.
- **Rationale**: The modeling logic improves methods that depend on relational
  understanding, but another top-level method would add routing and vocabulary
  cost without a distinct Task-level control return. Embedding preserves the
  behavior-changing insight while keeping the foundational basis and Human
  collaboration surface small.
- **Consequences**:
  - Later Working Method designs should use the logic when representation
    failure is causal to their own failure, not copy it by analogy.
  - `Model` remains progressive Agent depth inside Explore unless real work
    establishes a stronger independent control need.
  - Independent derivation continues with `Generate` before any cross-method
    generalization.
- **Reopen trigger**: Repeated high-value failures show that an explicitly
  selected Model Working Method improves routing, return quality, or
  collaboration enough to repay its additional common-ground and selection
  cost.

## D-062 — Generate Is Embedded Candidate-Space Logic With a Set-Level Return

- **State**: accepted for design; durable corpus landing and real-task effect
  pending
- **Authority and date**: Sir, 2026-08-08
- **Accepts**: `V-148..V-149` and
  [`design/52`](../design/52-generate-logic.md)
- **Decision**:
  - Do not create a top-level foundational `Generate` Working Method. Embed
    consequential candidate-space expansion in an applicable Working Method
    when missing candidates are causal to its failure.
  - Explore may retain `Generate` as an Agent-facing Route/job handle that
    distinguishes candidate shortage from missing evidence. It creates no
    method state or required Human vocabulary.
  - Judge the return at the candidate-set level: relevance, consequential
    difference, plausible-enough admissibility, and risk-relative coverage.
    Candidate count or surface-language variance does not establish adequacy.
  - Break shared lineage through a meaningfully different route when fixation
    cost warrants it. Keep generation-time evaluation light enough to preserve
    breadth while enforcing hard constraints and rejecting irrelevant,
    duplicate, or unusably vague candidates.
  - Return the set to its consumer. Design, Discriminate, Verification, and
    Human authority retain their respective selection, qualification, taste,
    and risk dispositions.
- **Rationale**: Candidate omission is a real and recurring failure, but its
  concrete candidates and validity conditions differ by owner. Embedded logic
  captures the set-level behavior without creating a central generator, fixed
  brainstorming procedure, or another common-ground object.
- **Consequences**:
  - Independent routes are conditional tactics, not automatic sub-agent
    delegation or required prompt variants.
  - Open-world residual remains explicit; adequate coverage never means proven
    exhaustiveness.
  - Independent derivation continues with Discriminate before accepting any
    further cross-method pattern.
- **Reopen trigger**: Candidate-space failures are repeatedly missed because
  the embedded logic is not discoverable, and a separately selected method
  improves terminal quality enough to repay routing and vocabulary cost.

## D-063 — Discriminate Is Embedded Candidate-Separation Logic

- **State**: accepted for design; durable corpus landing and real-task effect
  pending
- **Authority and date**: Sir, 2026-08-08
- **Accepts**: `V-152..V-153` and
  [`design/53`](../design/53-discriminate-logic.md)
- **Decision**:
  - Do not create a top-level foundational `Discriminate` Working Method.
    Embed candidate-separation logic where an applicable method faces material
    competing candidates; Explore may keep an optional Agent-facing Route/job
    handle without making it required Human common ground.
  - State what consequential candidates predict differently, then choose the
    cheapest reliable authorized observation whose possible outcomes change
    or secure the consuming action. Evidence volume without predicted
    separation is not a useful substitute.
  - Return a supported separation or decision-useful residual without forcing
    a winner. Several survivors count as action-equivalent only relative to the
    current purpose, scope, time horizon, loss tolerance, and authorized next
    action; longer-horizon or environmental consequences invalidate the
    equivalence.
  - Preserve observational equivalence and bounded incompleteness honestly.
    An intervention is optional and must still be safe, authorized, and worth
    its total cost.
  - Keep Discriminate and Verification semantically distinct even when they
    reuse one test, trace, probe, or observation. Discriminate resolves current
    alternatives; Verification qualifies a consequential expectation or claim.
    Neither grants effect, value, risk-acceptance, or final acceptance authority.
- **Rationale**: Candidate-relative prediction changes evidence selection and
  stopping behavior across many methods, but it does not own an independent
  Task-level return. Embedding preserves that leverage while avoiding another
  selected method, and the Verification boundary prevents epistemic resolution
  from masquerading as product/technical proof.
- **Consequences**:
  - The accepted Model, Generate, and Discriminate results must now be
    reintegrated into Explore to test whether Explore itself still earns
    foundational status and how little specialist vocabulary its core needs.
  - Later Verification design must retain claim-relative qualification even
    where its best evidence path also separates competing explanations.
  - Real tasks must test whether embedded discrimination remains discoverable
    and whether action-equivalence is applied too loosely.
- **Reopen trigger**: Repeated failures show that embedded guidance is routinely
  missed, that alternative resolution and claim qualification require a shared
  stronger contract, or that separately selected discrimination materially
  improves results enough to repay its routing/common-ground cost.

## D-064 — Working Methods Are Agent-Facing; Human Collaboration Uses Task Semantics

- **State**: accepted for design; durable corpus landing and real-task effect
  pending
- **Authority and date**: Sir, 2026-08-08
- **Refines**: the Human-projection clauses of `D-057`, `D-059`, and `D-060`;
  accepts `V-158`
- **Decision**:
  - Working Methods, their names, Route taxonomy, embedded logic, and guidance
    are Agent-operational knowledge, not a normal Human collaboration surface
    or required Human-Agent common ground.
  - Human collaboration exposes task semantics: purpose, consequential current
    action or change, evidence and material uncertainty, requests for
    information/resources, authority boundaries, and decisions. When an Agent
    explains its next move, use ordinary task language; do not require the Human
    to identify or monitor the underlying Working Method.
  - Do not progressively disclose Working Method vocabulary merely because a
    decision, resource request, or redirect becomes consequential. Disclose the
    consequential reason and effect instead.
  - Meta-level work that explicitly designs, audits, or debugs SVC/Agent
    behavior may discuss Working Methods directly. This is a different consumer
    context, not the ordinary task-facing projection.
- **Rationale**: Even optional method narration assumes a collaboration seam
  that ordinary Human work does not need. Translating only task consequences
  reduces common-ground and task-switching cost without hiding the information
  necessary for Human authority or intervention.
- **Consequences**:
  - Method discoverability and composition are Agent DX/corpus concerns.
  - `packet.md` and conversational updates should project consequential Task
    state, not method taxonomy or posture status.
  - Human predictability must not be used as a reason to expose Working Method
    vocabulary; it comes from understandable task behavior and decision points.
- **Reopen trigger**: Representative tasks show that one stable method name
  materially lowers recurring Human coordination cost compared with ordinary
  task language, without requiring method-system onboarding or monitoring.

## D-065 — Explore Is a Foundational Working Method for Non-Obvious Information Needs

- **State**: accepted for design; durable corpus landing and real-task effect
  pending
- **Authority and date**: Sir, 2026-08-09
- **Accepts and refines**: `V-156..V-160` and
  [`design/54`](../design/54-explore-foundational-method-reconciliation.md)
- **Decision**:
  - Keep **Explore** as a foundational Working Method. Finding key information
    is a prerequisite of and recurring need throughout almost every kind of
    Task and Step; Explore's relevance, information-move, and sufficiency logic
    remains stable enough to compose inside those different consumers.
  - Bound Explore to relatively ambiguous or complex information needs where
    what is key, where/how to obtain it, how sources relate, or which candidate
    explanation holds is not already obvious. Defect diagnosis, cross-repo
    reconciliation, and multi-source research are characteristic cases.
  - Treat direct query/retrieval with a sufficiently clear target and useful
    path as an ordinary operation, not a separate foundational method and not a
    reason to invoke Explore ceremony. Queries may still be tactics inside
    Explore, and their results may reopen it.
  - Keep the minimum core as three coupled judgments: provisional relevance,
    a consequential/proportionate information move, and return adequacy against
    continuation value. Preserve Model, Generate, and Discriminate as embedded
    progressive logic.
  - Explore is stateless and non-ritual. Its guidance may be consulted or set
    aside at any point and recur anywhere in the work; this creates no pause or
    exit lifecycle state. Returning control while information remains
    insufficient still exposes supported partial results and material residual.
- **Rationale**: Ubiquity alone could justify an empty universal wrapper, but
  Explore also has a distinctive return, stable failure pressure, bounded owner,
  economical simple-case boundary, and behavior-changing control logic. The
  query boundary prevents foundational status from turning all information
  access into ceremony.
- **Consequences**:
  - Prefer “find/obtain key information” over “collect information” in current
    guidance so filtering, synthesis, modeling, and discrimination remain part
    of the return rather than rewarding evidence volume.
  - Explore is not a front-loaded Task phase; Design, Implementation,
    Verification, Retrospective, and other work may use it recursively.
  - The next Working Method candidate must be derived from its own recurring
    situations rather than made a peer merely to complete a catalog.
- **Reopen trigger**: Real tasks show Explore is too broad to change behavior,
  its control is duplicated more economically by every consumer, the query
  boundary is not usable, or its total routing/reading cost exceeds the wrong-
  context and stopping failures it prevents.

## D-066 — Every Working Method Is Stateless and Non-Ritual

- **State**: accepted for design; durable corpus landing and real-task effect
  pending
- **Authority and date**: Sir, 2026-08-09
- **Generalizes**: `D-057` and the Explore-specific wording in `D-065`; accepts
  `V-164`
- **Decision**:
  - Every Working Method is a stateless tool/guidance surface, not a lifecycle.
    It creates no enter, active, pause, switch, resume, or exit state.
  - An Agent may consult, compose, stop using, or return to a method at any
    point where it helps. `Use when`, local validity, sufficiency, and return
    semantics describe correct use; they do not create transition ceremony.
  - Setting a method aside never satisfies, cancels, or hides the owning Task,
    Plan, Slice, semantic, authority, or effect obligation. State remains with
    those owners, not with the method.
  - When control is returned while the method-relative need remains materially
    unsatisfied, preserve the supported partial result, residual, consequence,
    and viable continuation through the applicable bounded-incomplete semantics.
    Do not require persistent write-back for every local cognitive move.
- **Rationale**: The non-lifecycle correction is structural, not Explore-
  specific. Applying it unevenly would recreate posture state and ceremony as
  soon as another method is introduced.
- **Consequences**:
  - Every later Working Method derivation is reviewed against this common
    principle rather than restating its own pause/exit protocol.
  - Method composition remains an Agent reasoning concern; Task and semantic
    owners preserve continuity and Human-visible consequences.
- **Reopen trigger**: A real method requires persistent execution state that
  cannot be owned more accurately by Task/work-control, semantic state, or an
  effect/authority mechanism, and the lifecycle lowers total failure cost.

## D-067 — Optimize SVC Corpus Wording for Semantic Compression

- **State**: accepted as a task-local corpus-writing principle; durable landing
  and mechanical enforcement pending
- **Authority and date**: Sir, 2026-08-09
- **Accepts and refines**: `V-165` and
  [`design/36`](../design/36-corpus-writing-standard-draft.md)
- **Decision**:
  - Choose SVC Corpus wording for high semantic payload at low total
    interpretation cost: one accurate, familiar word should replace several
    words when it preserves the needed meaning and excludes likely misreadings.
  - Treat “token efficiency” as semantic compression, not literal tokenizer-
    token minimization. Count ambiguity, wrong action, rereading, retrieval,
    translation, correction, and version-drift cost alongside text length.
  - Prefer wording that evokes the correct behavior. For example, “find/obtain
    key information” preserves filtering and synthesis, while “collect” can
    reward evidence volume; `Use when` avoids lifecycle implications carried by
    `activate` or `enter`.
  - Do not compress into obscure jargon, overloaded terms, or omitted scope,
    authority, exception, causal relation, or residual. A longer phrase is more
    efficient when it prevents material reconstruction or error.
- **Rationale**: Corpus words are control inputs to an Agent as well as prose.
  Their value depends on the behavior and distinctions they carry, not only
  bytes, words, or model tokens saved.
- **Consequences**:
  - The corpus-writing draft now owns this principle and should use examples
    discovered during later capability design.
  - Automation may check stable terminology or redundancy only after repeated,
    mechanically decidable failures; it cannot score semantic compression in
    general.
- **Reopen trigger**: Representative Agent/Human readings show the compressed
  wording loses necessary distinctions or that a more explicit phrase produces
  lower total interpretation and correction cost.

## D-068 — Design Consumes Typed Forces and Returns One Coupled Solution Distinct from Implementation Planning

- **State**: accepted for task-local design; planning relation and
  representation refined by `D-069`; foundational status, durable landing, and
  real-task effect remain open
- **Authority and date**: Sir, 2026-08-09
- **Accepts**: `V-167..V-170` and
  [`design/56`](../design/56-design-input-output-and-plan-seam.md)
- **Decision**:
  - Design consumes typed forces rather than one frozen requirement bundle:
    current/external reality, product intent and requirements, technical and
    deployment expectations, resource/economic constraints, and taste/design
    principles have different truth, freshness, authority, and correction
    behavior.
  - Design returns one coherent solution: proposed commitments about future
    product behavior and technical realization. `Proposed` preserves the
    applicable Human, stakeholder, product, or technical decision authority.
  - Product Design and Technical Design are coupled projections of that one
    solution, not mandatory independent stages or files. They may separate as
    artifacts only under useful consumer, owner, scale, review, or freshness
    pressure.
  - Design owns what future product/system should exist and why it can realize
    intended effects. Implementation planning owns how current reality will be
    transformed into it through Slice/Step sequencing, dependencies,
    assignments, integration, and effect order. Transition semantics belong to
    Design; the controlled work sequence that realizes them belongs to Plan.
  - The current closest essence is: **Design shapes possible futures into a
    coherent solution—proposed product and technical commitments that can
    realize intended effects within current reality, constraints, and resource
    horizons.** It is the working definition, not a claim that the final corpus
    wording or foundational status has already been settled.
- **Rationale**: Treating every input as fixed truth hides authority and
  correction; independently designing product and technology permits semantic
  divergence; calling Design “planning” collapses solution commitments into
  work sequencing. The accepted model separates these failure surfaces while
  preserving the familiar output word “solution/方案”.
- **Consequences**:
  - Analysis is generic reasoning, Discussion is a collaboration interface,
    and only material authority decisions leave Design for the applicable
    Decision/Human owner; none becomes a mandatory Design stage.
  - Implementation feedback may reopen either Plan or Design depending on
    whether it falsifies local execution or a solution commitment.
  - The next Design question is how much solution resolution is sufficient for
    implementation planning without pre-deciding cheap, reversible, local
    implementation freedom.
- **Reopen trigger**: Real tasks show the typed distinctions add classification
  cost without changing behavior, the coupled projections prevent useful
  independent ownership, the Design/Plan seam cannot classify transition or
  prototype work, or commitment-shaping systematically misses invention,
  embodiment, or aesthetic judgment.

## D-069 — Design Adequacy Is Consumer-Relative; Representation Follows Collaboration and Memory

- **State**: accepted for task-local design; durable landing and real-task
  effect remain open
- **Authority and date**: Sir, 2026-08-09
- **Accepts and corrects**: `V-171..V-174` and
  [`design/57`](../design/57-design-resolution-and-representation.md)
- **Decision**:
  - “Sufficient for the current implementation horizon” describes whether a
    Design return can support that consumer without hiding a material solution
    decision. It is not a Design handoff, pause, exit, readiness state, or
    pipeline barrier. Design remains a stateless method that may be used again
    anywhere; the owning Task and semantic obligations remain.
  - Materiality follows product/system consequence, multi-consumer coupling,
    observability, loss, and reversibility—not abstraction level, file type, or
    code-versus-document form. Resolve consequential commitments before
    dependent work; preserve cheap, bounded, reversible implementation freedom.
  - Design resolution and implementation-Plan horizon may co-evolve. A partial
    solution can support bounded implementation while material residuals remain
    contained and explicit; an unresolved boundary may return to Design or
    become planned evidence/prototype work without demanding a complete upfront
    specification.
  - Design as an activity does not create a document requirement. Persistent
    representation is justified when meaning must survive collaboration or
    memory boundaries among Humans, Agents, contexts, or time. Choose prose,
    table, topology/sequence diagram, pseudocode, prototype, executable code,
    or another carrier by total communication, reconstruction, challenge,
    maintenance, and loss cost for the actual consumers and information shape.
  - Code/prototypes are possible carriers, not privileged ones. Sir's intended
    emphasis is closer to pseudocode and representation fitness: for example, a
    Mermaid sequence diagram may communicate a deeply nested payment flow more
    efficiently and accurately than a much longer list.
- **Rationale**: Treating planning adequacy as an exit condition would recreate
  the Working-Method lifecycle rejected in `D-066`. Treating documentation as a
  property of Design confuses the work that produces meaning with the Human/
  Agent coordination and memory boundaries across which that meaning must
  remain usable.
- **Consequences**:
  - Prefer “supports the current implementation horizon” over “is handed off to
    implementation planning” where the latter suggests a phase transition.
  - The task-local corpus-writing draft now extends semantic compression from
    word choice to information-carrier choice; it does not mandate Mermaid or a
    Design artifact.
  - Foundational-method review must assess Design's distinctive return and
    behavior, not whether it owns a persistent document or lifecycle.
- **Reopen trigger**: Real tasks show horizon-relative adequacy still causes a
  hidden stage gate, materiality cannot be judged usefully, or carrier choice
  adds format churn without lowering collaboration, reconstruction, memory, or
  decision cost.

## D-070 — Design Is the Second Foundational Working Method

- **State**: accepted at capability-model depth; exact corpus landing,
  progressive design/taste guidance, and real-task effect remain open
- **Authority and date**: Sir, 2026-08-09
- **Accepts**: `V-175..V-177` and
  [`design/58`](../design/58-design-foundational-admission.md)
- **Decision**:
  - Design is the second foundational Working Method after Explore. Its
    distinctive return is one coherent solution: proposed product and technical
    commitments at the resolution useful to the current consumer, with material
    residuals preserved.
  - Its primitive topology relates typed intent/reality/constraints/resources/
    taste, proposed solution commitments, and representative product/system
    consequences. Any side may revise another; this is not a linear process.
  - Design remains stateless and non-ritual. It has no activation, handoff,
    pause, exit, readiness gate, mandatory alternative count, or required
    document. Task and semantic obligations remain with their actual owners.
  - Explore, Model, Generate, Discriminate, Decision, diagrams, pseudocode,
    prototypes, specialist taste, and implementation feedback can support
    Design but do not individually own its coherent-solution synthesis.
  - A proposed solution does not grant Human/stakeholder authority or establish
    Verification success; Plan owns work sequencing, Implementation owns
    realization, and cheap bounded reversible choices remain implementation
    freedom.
- **Rationale**: Leaving Design as an unnamed composition does not eliminate
  solution shaping. It removes the owner that keeps product and technical
  commitments coherent across local choices, so Plan or the first
  implementation tends to acquire accidental design authority. `Design` is a
  familiar, semantically compressed name for a stable management-useful return.
- **Consequences**:
  - The Working Protocol capability model now has two accepted foundational
    methods: Explore and Design. It does not yet have an exhaustive method list.
  - The next question is how Design selects and applies general reasoning,
    decision lenses, representations, and specialist taste without bloating its
    primitive core or making valuable guidance too easy to miss.
  - Tastes & Design Ability remains a separate functional cluster: foundation
    acceptance does not claim the primitive alone teaches excellent UI/UX,
    architecture, or implementation judgment.
- **Reopen trigger**: Representative tasks show Design guidance adds ceremony,
  premature commitments, documents, or context load without improving solution
  coherence, terminal product/system quality, Human alignment, or future change
  cost relative to ordinary planning and implementation.
