# Decisions D-001..D-010

## D-001 — Target Human Collaboration Unit

- **State**: accepted
- **Authority and date**: Sir, 2026-07-29
- **Decision**: Design for one Human as the most common case and approximately
  three to five Humans as the upper ordinary team size, plus Coding Agents
  admitted according to work pressure and integration return.
- **Rationale**: SVC should multiply a small team's ability rather than assume a
  large coordination organization. Agent count is not a fixed quota.
- **Consequences**:
  - One-Human usability and control cost are first-class constraints.
  - Multi-Human authority and handoff must remain coherent without introducing
    enterprise role bureaucracy.
  - Agent orchestration must optimize net integration return rather than agent
    utilization.
- **Reopen trigger**: SVC deliberately targets materially larger Human teams or
  organizational governance.

## D-002 — Meaning of Large-Software Support

- **State**: accepted
- **Authority and date**: Sir, 2026-07-29
- **Decision**: “Large software” refers to system complexity, breadth,
  longevity, and lifecycle—not to large Human-team size.
- **Rationale**: A one-to-five-person Human team with Coding Agents must be able
  to preserve product, technical, operational, and task coherence across a
  system too large to hold in one person's or one context window's memory.
- **Consequences**:
  - Product, contract, unit, deployment, migration, rollout, and evidence
    topology matter more than portfolio or staffing structures.
  - Large-system support may span repositories, but must retain canonical owner
    references rather than copy a global truth graph into task state.
  - Evaluation must include lifecycle continuity and cross-boundary change, not
    only one implementation episode.
- **Reopen trigger**: The intended product expands into portfolio, staffing, or
  enterprise program management.

## D-008 — Working Outcome Scales and Inquiry Topology

- **State**: accepted
- **Authority and date**: Sir, 2026-07-29
- **Decision**:
  - Preserve Human-Agent collaboration effectiveness, Agent long-task
    effectiveness, and low-cost large-system development/evolution as distinct
    outcome scales. Do not assume a hierarchy among them.
  - Organize inquiry as a feedback loop from real Consumer episode and desired
    result through current path and competing causes, observable capabilities,
    candidate mechanisms, vertical rehearsal, quality/cost evidence, and only
    then SVC/DX projection.
  - Treat task packets, sub-agent orchestration, working/reasoning modes, system
    models, CLI, validation, and telemetry as candidate mechanisms or
    projections rather than top-level outcome categories.
- **Rationale**: The previous mechanism-first map replaced the three requested
  outcomes with narrower properties and could not support a faithful design
  discussion. Sir confirmed the corrected outcome and inquiry model.
- **Consequences**:
  - The first active inquiry uses a real episode to construct a failure and
    causal model before selecting a capability or mechanism.
  - Evaluation includes actual output quality and total Human, Agent, rework,
    coordination, and lifecycle cost from the beginning.
  - A scenario contradiction may reopen only the affected part of this working
    model.
- **Reopen trigger**: The three-scale interpretation hides a material fourth
  outcome, real cases do not fit the feedback topology, or Sir finds that the
  map again constrains judgment through solution-biased categories.

## D-009 — Gleaning-Led, Execution-Evolved Design Method

- **State**: accepted
- **Authority and date**: Sir, 2026-07-29
- **Decision**:
  - Sir may provide partial, heterogeneous, and uncertain gleanings rather than
    a complete theory or causal framework.
  - The Lead Agent is responsible for integrating them toward the accepted
    outcomes, separating insight from hypothesis, challenging boundaries,
    proposing a minimal coherent mechanism, and exposing only consequential
    trade-offs for Human review or decision.
  - SVC should evolve through bounded real-task use and observed results rather
    than wait for a complete paper design.
  - Progressive disclosure, simple and direct unambiguous language, low
    redundancy, and intuitive structure are standing design constraints.
  - Provisional models also pay cognitive and maintenance cost. Manage them by
    marginal complexity return: preserve useful explanatory structure,
    simplify low-value detail, and elaborate only when a concrete decision
    needs it. Neither wholesale adoption nor wholesale rejection is the default.
- **Rationale**: Requiring Sir to diagnose the framework or answer abstract
  causal questions transfers the system-design burden back to the Human and
  works against the collaboration-efficiency objective. Real execution is
  needed to expose costs and failures that discussion alone cannot settle.
- **Consequences**:
  - Gleanings enter as accepted inputs or hypotheses according to their stated
    confidence, never as automatically accepted SVC rules.
  - Candidate mechanisms must be reversible and pressure-triggered enough to
    learn from actual tasks.
  - Real tasks need not be selected or shaped to validate a provisional model;
    naturally occurring evidence may refine it when useful.
  - Discussion briefs ask Sir for facts, taste, and material trade-offs, not for
    a theory the Lead Agent should construct.
- **Reopen trigger**: Iterative experiments create uncontrolled Consumer cost,
  the Lead synthesis repeatedly misrepresents intent, or a high-risk change
  requires stronger evidence before any trial.

## D-010 — Personal and Opinionated Taste Target

- **State**: accepted
- **Authority and date**: Sir, 2026-08-03
- **Decision**:
  - SVC primarily serves Sir's personal software-development experience rather
    than optimizing for taste neutrality across unrelated Consumers.
  - SVC should contain substantive implementation taste, including UI/UX,
    architecture, and other product and technical design judgment, so an Agent
    can usually work in alignment with Sir's thinking.
  - Alignment is not obedience. When an Agent has a materially better idea, it
    should expose the proposed departure, rationale, trade-offs, and relevant
    evidence in terms Sir can judge.
- **Rationale**: A neutral process framework would leave a central part of the
  desired collaboration outcome implicit and force Sir to repeatedly restate
  preferences. Opinionated defaults can improve both output quality and
  collaboration efficiency when their meaning and applicability are legible.
- **Consequences**:
  - Sir's explicit stable preference is valid authority for a taste claim even
    when it is not a universal engineering truth.
  - Generality is not an intrinsic benefit. A concrete method or guideline may
    enter SVC because it fits Sir's preferred way of building, subject to its
    context, cost, and interaction with other taste.
  - Taste needs a progressive-disclosure and application method, not only a
    list of slogans: an Agent must know when a principle applies, what it
    optimizes, what it costs, and when a departure merits Human review.
  - Accepted examples, rejected outputs, and real-task corrections may refine
    taste, but one local correction does not automatically become a durable
    universal rule.
- **Reopen trigger**: SVC deliberately becomes a taste-neutral framework for
  unrelated Consumers, or Sir chooses to separate personal taste from SVC's
  canonical content.
