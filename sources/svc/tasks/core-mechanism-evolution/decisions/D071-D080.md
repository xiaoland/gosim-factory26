# Decisions D-071..D-080

## D-071 — Design Integrates Progressive Guidance and Specialist Taste Through an Ownership Seam

- **State**: accepted at capability-model depth; exact source layout,
  specialist content, retrieval behavior, and real-task effect remain open
- **Authority and date**: Sir, 2026-08-09
- **Accepts**: `V-178..V-181` and
  [`design/59`](../design/59-design-guidance-and-taste-seam.md)
- **Decision**:
  - Keep Design's foundational primitive small while giving it practical
    judgment through progressive depth. Design owns selection, composition, and
    integration of relevant lenses into one coherent solution; it does not
    become the semantic owner of every law or specialist claim it consumes.
  - Cross-working authority, evidence, stakeholder, and economic/ROI rules keep
    their applicable universal or Decision/Human owners. Design uses them when
    shaping and challenging a solution.
  - Progressive Design guidance owns recurring general solution-shaping
    techniques such as operational first-principles reconstruction, structural/
    temporal representation, representative-consequence and counterfactual
    challenge, change-force analysis, and option/reversibility reasoning.
  - Tastes & Design Ability owns substantive domain quality models, bounded
    heuristics, examples/counterexamples, and preferences for product, UI/UX,
    architecture, data, deployment, and implementation. Design consumes that
    depth under relevant pressure.
  - Personal product/aesthetic preference carries Sir's authority. Expert taste
    such as deep-module or complexity heuristics is rebuttable guidance whose
    scope and predicted consequences must fit evidence, stakeholders, and ROI.
  - Judge placement by expected task effect: relevant-guidance retrieval and
    solution-quality gain versus omission, irrelevant-context/ritual,
    integration, reconciliation, and maintenance cost. Prefer a small entry
    with pressure-routed depth, but not when fragmented retrieval performs worse
    than a coherent surface.
- **Rationale**: A primitive-only Design method prevents ownership loss but does
  not teach excellent judgment. A monolithic Design handbook loads irrelevant
  domains, encourages checklist behavior, and collapses claim owners. The seam
  gives Design practical “soul” while preserving progressive disclosure and
  limits at several layers.
- **Consequences**:
  - Design is complete at current Working-Protocol capability depth; later `TD`
    work supplies and challenges specialist content without reopening the
    primitive unless the seam fails.
  - The current `implementation-taste.md` is a software-implementation foothold,
    not the assumed owner for all product/UI/UX/architecture guidance. Exact
    new surfaces wait for the `TD` capability and landing discussion.
  - A future authorized Working Protocol landing should expose a compact Design
    bootstrap and progressive routes, not copy specialist guidance into the
    universal entry.
- **Reopen trigger**: Representative tasks show the layered seam routinely
  misses relevant guidance, produces contradictory duplicated claims, costs
  more to route/integrate than one coherent surface, or fails to improve
  solution quality while still adding taxonomy and context.

## D-072 — Implementation Boundary and Realization-Feedback Core Are Accepted

- **State**: accepted at boundary depth; foundational admission and the full
  Verification/Executor seams remain open
- **Authority and date**: Sir, 2026-08-09
- **Accepts**: `V-182..V-184` and
  [`design/60`](../design/60-implementation-foundational-method.md)
- **Decision**:
  - The distinctive Implementation concern is making a bounded intended change
    actual in product/system artifacts or runtime state. It is not an `IM`
    Slice tag, Plan, Executor role, mutation permission, command/patch, or proof.
  - The potentially valuable reusable behavior is a development loop: change
    actual state, obtain discriminating realization feedback, then adjust the
    implementation or return a mismatch that requires solution revision.
  - Direct edits, deterministic transformations, adaptive development, and
    migration steps are valid compressed or expanded instances of that model.
  - Implementation planning remains reopenable under the Task Packet model;
    the loop does not imply that every iteration becomes a Plan Step.
  - Executor remains a later sub-agent design. Specialization around a bounded
    feedback loop is retained as a cross-cluster pattern rather than used to
    define Implementation by role.
  - Verification design, in-loop feedback, independent qualification, tests,
    and Human acceptance need a later owner analysis; their co-occurrence does
    not collapse them into one Implementation step.
- **Rationale**: The boundary preserves one concern across materially different
  realization cases while avoiding file-edit, actor, planning, authority, and
  proof conflation. The loop candidate explains why actual reality can revise
  both tactics and solution commitments.
- **Reopen trigger**: Further derivation shows that “actualized change” cannot
  be distinguished from Verification success, or that generic protocol plus
  Slice/Executor guidance produces the same behavior at lower common-ground
  and maintenance cost.

## D-073 — Implementation Is the Third Foundational Working Method

- **State**: accepted at Working-Protocol capability depth; progressive
  Implementation guidance, Executor realization, detailed Verification, and
  real-task effect remain open
- **Authority and date**: Sir, 2026-08-09
- **Accepts**: `V-185..V-187` and
  [`design/61`](../design/61-implementation-development-loop-and-admission.md)
- **Decision**:
  - Implementation is the third foundational Working Method after Explore and
    Design. Its purpose is to make a bounded intended product/system change
    actual under real implementation feedback.
  - Its primitive topology is `bounded intent + current reality ↔
    proportionate change ↔ discriminating realization feedback`, with local
    adjustment or an honest return when reality falsifies the solution, scope,
    or remaining obligation.
  - Its return is an actualized bounded change, material realization feedback,
    and unresolved obligations. `Actualized` does not mean `qualified`;
    independent Verification owns whether the resulting claims hold.
  - Clear local work compresses the method into direct change plus a cheap
    observation. No implementation phase, lifecycle, method state, artifact,
    loop transcript, mandatory test ceremony, or Executor role is implied.
  - Implementation Plan and `IM` Slice semantics remain owned by Task Packet;
    loop iterations become Steps only when their externalized control state has
    net management value.
- **Rationale**: Actual construction exposes reality-specific friction that
  neither a solution nor a Plan can fully precompute. A shared development-loop
  method changes direct, deterministic, adaptive, migration, and single-Agent
  behavior without making an Executor role own the reason to respond to that
  friction.
- **Reopen trigger**: Representative use shows the method does not improve
  implementation behavior, routinely turns cheap changes into ceremony, or
  cannot preserve the actualized/qualified distinction in Agent practice.

## D-074 — Verification Is a Capability, Not a Foundational Working Method

- **State**: accepted at Working-Protocol category depth; detailed `VF`
  capability, verifier guidance, acceptance, and real-task effect remain open
- **Authority and date**: Sir, 2026-08-09
- **Accepts**: `V-191..V-195` and
  [`design/63`](../design/63-verification-capability-and-trusted-verifier.md)
- **Decision**:
  - Do not admit Verification as a fourth foundational Working Method.
  - Retain Verification as a cross-cutting capability, semantic qualification
    concern, possible `VR` Slice return, trusted-verifier architecture,
    progressive specialist guidance, and evidence/integration Guardrails.
  - The prior `claim/scope → discriminating observation → proportionate
    evidence → disposition` topology is Verification-solution Design guidance.
    It does not describe a distinct method after a verifier exists.
  - Existing verifier execution is an ordinary action plus protocol
    integration. Missing verifier uses Design and Implementation; ambiguous
    evidence uses Explore/Discriminate; verdict reliance uses applicable
    authority and effect/acceptance semantics.
  - Prefer qualified external deterministic mechanisms when they faithfully
    encode the claim, but do not equate determinism with validity. The TCB can
    include specification, input integrity, oracle, instrumentation/environment,
    verdict interpretation, residual scope, and effect gate.
- **Rationale**: The decomposition preserves the important return and system
  capability while avoiding a redundant method name. It also reconnects the
  proof-carrying sub-agent model to a small, challengeable TCB rather than trust
  in the producer.
- **Reopen trigger**: Representative tasks reveal a stable, non-trivial
  qualification behavior that cannot be economically decomposed into Design,
  Implementation, Explore/Discriminate, verifier execution, and protocol/
  authority integration.

## D-075 — Three Foundations Are Provisionally Complete; Derive Retrospective as Composed Closing Guidance

- **State**: accepted at current capability depth; basis remains reopenable by
  non-composable behavior and real use
- **Authority and date**: Sir, 2026-08-09
- **Accepts**: `V-196..V-197` and
  [`design/64`](../design/64-foundational-working-method-basis-audit.md)
- **Decision**:
  - Treat Explore, Design, and Implementation as the provisionally complete
    foundational Working Method basis.
  - Do not add Decision, Planning, Review, Verification, Diagnosis,
    consolidation, or Retrospective merely because each is important or has a
    recognizable activity/return name.
  - Retain their actual owners: protocol/authority, Task Packet control,
    specialist review/verifier design, mismatch-framed Explore, semantic-owner
    integration, or composed guidance as applicable.
  - Derive Agent work-system Retrospective next. Its accepted consumer chain is
    Task evidence → supported adaptation opportunity → Design/Implementation →
    future Agent/work system → later effect/counterevidence.
  - Because this is still a new and weakly practiced area, keep the model
    low-commitment, evidence-bounded, and free of mandatory automation,
    lifecycle, artifacts, or claimed best practice.
- **Rationale**: The audit currently finds three distinct transformations—
  supported understanding, possible solution, and actual state—while the other
  candidates decompose without disappearing. Retrospective has the strongest
  unresolved specialized procedure and can still falsify the basis.
- **Reopen trigger**: Decision, Retrospective, or another real case exposes a
  stable management-useful transformation that cannot be economically composed
  from the three foundations and protocol mechanisms.

## D-076 — Retrospective Is Composed, Pressure-Triggered Closing Guidance

- **State**: accepted at capability-model depth; real-task effect and exact
  corpus landing remain open
- **Authority and date**: Sir, 2026-08-09
- **Accepts**: `V-198..V-201` and
  [`design/65`](../design/65-agent-work-system-retrospective-guidance.md)
- **Decision**:
  - Agent work-system Retrospective is not a fourth foundational Working
    Method. It is pressure-triggered composed closing guidance using Explore,
    Design, and Implementation.
  - Its minimum discipline is: identify a material plausibly avoidable loss;
    diagnose the earliest modifiable work-system relation; state a behavioral
    counterfactual and smallest credible intervention; define later evidence
    that can retain, revise, or retire it.
  - Its normal returns are `no adaptation` or a supported adaptation
    opportunity. The latter is immediately consumed by Design/Implementation
    and applicable authority; a future Agent/work system is the terminal
    beneficiary, and future matching work supplies effect/counterevidence.
  - Raw resource/activity counts and same-context self-narrative are signals or
    hypotheses, not waste or causal proof. Necessary exploration and one-off
    difficulty must remain possible.
  - `RT` names a Slice scope when management value exists; it does not imply a
    method, lifecycle, default `retrospective.md`, hook, score, memory append,
    automatic mutation, or separate Task.
  - Project-truth consolidation remains a distinct closing concern.
- **Rationale**: The model preserves the desired future-behavior consumer chain
  and intervention/subtraction economics without claiming mature industry
  evidence or building an append-only self-improvement system.
- **Reopen trigger**: Real use shows the four questions fail to guide useful
  adaptation, no-op is systematically missed, valuable patterns require stable
  state/artifacts, or the added reflection cost does not improve future task
  outcomes enough to repay itself.

## D-077 — Project Truth Integrates Continuously and Closes Through a Residual Check

- **State**: accepted at capability-model depth; exact corpus landing and real-
  task reliability remain open
- **Authority and date**: Sir, 2026-08-10
- **Accepts**: `V-202..V-204` and
  [`design/66`](../design/66-project-truth-consolidation-seam.md)
- **Decision**:
  - Project-truth consolidation is distinct from Agent work-system
    Retrospective but not a symmetric post-Task stage. Integrate accepted
    semantic deltas when they become sufficiently stable and authorized.
  - Before formal closure at a declared truth horizon, always perform a cheap
    residual check: no accepted material semantic delta may remain only in
    volatile task state or materially contradict the canonical owner future
    consumers will trust.
  - No residual creates no artifact or history scan. An actionable residual
    becomes meaningful `IQ`/`DS`/`IM`/`VR` Plan work; a blocked residual must
    expose consequence, owner/authority need, and viable continuation, allowing
    closure only at a lower honest horizon.
  - Plan the semantic change, evidence/Decision basis, owner/consumers,
    reconciliation, authority, and qualification—not a target document path.
  - Promotion means updating the correct canonical owner, which may be code,
    configuration, schema, test, automation, or durable documentation. Do not
    promote every finding into docs.
  - Do not add a Consolidate method/tag, `consolidation.md`, closing Phase,
    promotion registry, or deletion hook. Packet deletion neither triggers nor
    proves consolidation.
- **Rationale**: Continuous owner integration prevents stale truth and shadow
  authority; the mandatory but compressed pre-close check catches stranded
  accepted meaning without turning closure into documentation bureaucracy.
- **Reopen trigger**: Agents mutate owners prematurely while meaning remains
  volatile, routinely forget stranded deltas without stronger control, or the
  check expands into expensive unrelated documentation audits.

## D-078 — Design Routes by Use Case and Gives Test Design an Independent, Claim-Dependent Projection

- **State**: accepted at capability-model depth; exact catalog, source shape,
  specialist content, and real-task effect remain open
- **Authority and date**: Sir, 2026-08-10
- **Accepts**: `V-205..V-208` with `V-207` refined, and
  [`design/67`](../design/67-design-use-case-routing-and-test-design.md)
- **Decision**:
  - Retain `D-071`'s small Design primitive and ownership seam, but route
    progressive guidance through Product Design, Technical Design, and local
    recurring design use cases rather than an inert methodology/taste shelf.
  - Admit a methodology or taste item only when a discoverable recurring use
    case, material predicted consequence, useful counter-pressure, and
    retrieval/maintenance ROI justify it.
  - Product Design, Technical Design, and Test Design are three independently
    managed projections of one coherent design solution; they are not phases,
    mandatory documents, or unrelated solutions.
  - Test Design independently owns how material claims are challenged and
    observed, including scenario, environment, observation surface, oracle,
    invariant, comparison, Human judgment, independence/TCB, and residual
    qualification.
  - Every Test Design expectation must trace to an owned Product or Technical
    claim. If none exists, return a missing/contradictory/unobservable
    specification rather than inventing a fixture, oracle, or requirement from
    the implementation under test.
  - A claim-free exploratory probe remains legitimate, but it returns evidence
    for Inquiry/Design; it cannot manufacture a normative pass/fail verdict or
    qualification horizon.
  - Keep local claim and testability pressure near each Product/Technical use
    case; load deeper Test Design guidance only when composition is non-trivial.
    Test automation or probe construction remains Implementation, and verifier
    execution remains Verification capability.
- **Rationale**: Use-case routing turns design knowledge into decision-time
  behavior. A separate Test Design projection preserves a consumer and review
  surface, while semantic traceability prevents tests from manufacturing the
  requirements they appear to prove.
- **Reopen trigger**: Representative work shows routing costs exceed solution
  gains, the three projections fragment one decision, claim traceability hides
  important emergent qualities, or embedded Test Design performs better than
  an independent management surface.

## D-079 — Universal Working Control Is a Set of Connections and Laws, Not Another SOP or Method

- **State**: accepted at capability-model depth; durable compression and real-
  task effect remain open
- **Authority and date**: Sir, 2026-08-10
- **Accepts**: `V-209..V-212` and
  [`design/68`](../design/68-universal-working-control-guidance.md)
- **Decision**:
  - Do not add a universal control SOP, lifecycle, status schema, or fourth
    foundational Working Method.
  - Preserve four universal connections: current obligation to bounded useful
    return; return to suitable method/action and discriminating feedback;
    action to independent effect authority; observation to integrated return,
    residual, and next disposition.
  - Treat authority and truth-corruption prevention as Working Laws: method,
    tag, confidence, or successful command does not grant effect authority;
    candidate output requires semantic and typed-authority integration;
    `found`, `designed`, `actualized`, `qualified`, and `accepted` do not imply
    one another; bounded-incomplete is not success.
  - Treat return size, method/feedback choice, and replanning as adaptive
    guidance governed by the current consumer, risk, evidence, and ROI.
  - Human-facing collaboration projects consequential outcomes/effects,
    decision-ready propositions, and honest handoff/closure rather than Working
    Method telemetry. Safe epistemic work remains autonomous.
- **Rationale**: The relations prevent activity drift, authority leakage,
  unintegrated candidate truth, and false completion across simple and complex
  Tasks without forcing ordinary work through a visible process ritual.
- **Reopen trigger**: Representative Tasks need another stable control relation,
  ordinary work pays material translation cost, or the model cannot guide
  action without becoming a schema/state machine.

## D-080 — Working Protocol Is an Operational Kernel and Navigation Entry, Not a Semantic Umbrella

- **State**: accepted at capability-model depth; exact source shape, migration,
  and real retrieval effect remain open
- **Authority and date**: Sir, 2026-08-10
- **Accepts**: `V-213..V-215` and
  [`design/69`](../design/69-working-protocol-current-target-and-domain-boundary.md)
- **Decision**:
  - Replace the current flat-umbrella model with an Agent-facing operational
    kernel that keeps Task obligation, useful return, authority, feedback,
    integration, Human collaboration, and honest disposition connected.
  - Working Protocol directly owns the universal control/Guardrails, Task
    Packet control and write-back seam, foundational Working Method entry,
    Human collaboration, integration/closing guidance, and specialist-routing
    contract.
  - It may be one compact navigation umbrella for non-trivial work, but it does
    not semantically own Task Packet shapes, Sub-agent methods, Verification
    depth, Tastes & Design content, corpus/document writing, or optional tool/
    extension behavior.
  - Its mechanism serves `O-INTERACTION` through predictable autonomy and
    typed Human authority, `O-TASK` through return/feedback/integration/replan,
    and `O-SYSTEM` through owner/effect/qualification/consolidation discipline;
    it is not a fourth outcome and cannot produce those outcomes alone.
  - The `Working Laws` wording in `D-079` describes universal binding force,
    not an accepted new corpus term. Preserve `D-059..D-060`'s common-ground
    economy by preferring universal/scoped Guardrails or plain invariant
    language unless a distinct `Working Law` concept later earns admission.
- **Rationale**: The current source co-locates concerns with different triggers,
  consumers, cadence, and proof paths. Kernel-plus-routing gives them causal
  relation and one cheap entry without recreating a monolithic semantic owner.
- **Reopen trigger**: Progressive routing scatters indispensable rules, Agents
  cannot discover specialist depth reliably, or one coherent owner/file
  performs better after retrieval and maintenance cost are counted.
