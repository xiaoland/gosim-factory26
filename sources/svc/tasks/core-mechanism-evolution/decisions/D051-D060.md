# Decisions D-051..D-060

## D-051 — Route Is an Explore Control Point, Not a Universal Posture Step

- **State**: accepted for design; strategy catalog and outcome evidence pending
- **Authority and date**: Sir, 2026-08-08
- **Refines**: `D-049` and `D-050`; accepts the topology in `V-121` without
  promoting its candidate strategies into a fixed catalog
- **Decision**:
  - After a non-trivial Frame, Explore selects an information-seeking strategy
    fitting the current information situation and proportionate total cost.
    Call this control point `Route`.
  - Results may invalidate or deepen the Frame, make another strategy more
    useful, or satisfy the useful return; Explore therefore does not prescribe
    one post-Frame pipeline.
  - `Route` is common to non-trivial Explore work, not automatically a step in
    every Working Posture. Generic method selection remains part of the
    universal protocol; another posture receives a named routing point only if
    it has a distinctive choice that prevents repeatable posture-specific loss.
  - Retrieve, Map, Discriminate, Discover, and Probe remain provisional
    contrasts for deriving progressively loaded methods. Their names,
    separation, and ownership are not accepted as a durable catalog.
  - Frame, Route, and the selected method may collapse into one direct move
    when the information need and fitting strategy are obvious.
- **Rationale**: One Frame can require qualitatively different ways of finding
  information. Making that choice visible when it is consequential prevents
  recurring method–question mismatches without imposing a lifecycle on cheap
  lookups or unrelated postures.
- **Consequences**:
  - The remaining Explore closure question is its activation and
    return/stop/switch contract, not another mandatory linear step.
  - Later posture designs may compare this conditional-routing structure but
    cannot copy `Route` by analogy alone.
  - Real Consumer Tasks must still establish whether explicit routing improves
    terminal results enough to repay selection and vocabulary cost.
- **Reopen trigger**: Representative tasks show that ordinary Agent judgment
  selects equally effective Explore methods at lower total cost, or that the
  strategy distinctions cannot be made without first doing most of the work.

## D-052 — Explore Core SOP Is Complete at Capability-Model Depth

- **State**: accepted for design; operational and outcome evidence pending
- **Authority and date**: Sir, 2026-08-08
- **Refines**: `D-047` and `D-049..D-051`; accepts the closure in `V-123`
- **Decision**:
  - Activate non-trivial Explore when reliable progress depends on resolving a
    material information need and neither the answer nor the fitting way to
    obtain it is already obvious. Keep direct lookup compressed.
  - Use Frame to define provisional relevance, Route to select a proportionate
    information-seeking method, and the resulting information to reframe,
    reroute, return, switch posture, or stop bounded-incomplete.
  - A successful return supplies the smallest answer, model, or discriminating
    observation that enables the Frame's purpose, together with material
    residual uncertainty. It does not substitute search logs, context volume,
    or source counts for key information.
  - Stop bounded-incomplete when needed information is unavailable,
    unauthorized, or not worth further total cost; preserve the supported
    result, material unknown, consequence, and best known next discriminator.
  - Treat the post-method junction as the Explore exit/feedback contract, not
    a third named Step. Feedback integration and replanning remain universal
    Working Protocol concerns.
- **Rationale**: The model now states when Explore applies, how to carry it out,
  and when it returns/stops/switches—the minimum SOP contract in `D-047`—without
  reconstructing a linear lifecycle.
- **Consequences**:
  - Explore is complete only at current capability-model depth. Strategy
    distinctions/SOPs, operational sufficiency judgment, and real-task effect
    remain open progressively loaded content.
  - The exact durable SVC landing remains part of the top-level Working Posture
    proposition, not an Explore-local decision.
- **Reopen trigger**: Strategy work reveals a missing common Explore control
  function, or the activation/exit contract does not materially guide Agent
  behavior in representative tasks.

## D-053 — Every Working Posture Supports Bounded-Incomplete Return

- **State**: accepted as a common Working Posture design pattern; exact
  posture-specific forms pending
- **Authority and date**: Sir, 2026-08-08
- **Generalizes**: one important part of `D-052` without copying Explore's
  Frame, Route, or information-specific return
- **Decision**:
  - A posture may return before satisfying its intended result when a required
    input/evidence/condition is unavailable, applicable authority is absent, or
    continuing has disproportionate expected total cost.
  - Bounded-incomplete is a valid control return but not success or acceptance.
    It must distinguish the supported partial result, the unsatisfied condition
    or material residual, its consequence for downstream use, and the best
    viable continuation condition/action/decision.
  - The caller or applicable Human authority decides how the partial return is
    integrated, deferred, redirected, or abandoned. The pattern does not grant
    an Agent authority to redefine intent, accept risk, or silently stop a Task.
  - Each posture must derive its own concrete boundary and return content; no
    universal field set or artifact is implied.
- **Rationale**: Without an explicit incomplete return, Agents tend to loop,
  inflate work until evidence looks sufficient, conceal unresolved risk, or
  report completion despite missing authority or prerequisites.
- **Consequences**:
  - Later posture SOP reviews must include a bounded-incomplete path alongside
    satisfied return and switching behavior.
  - Task Packet state can project the material residual when it affects current
    control, but the pattern does not create a new information module.
- **Reopen trigger**: A posture has no meaningful partial result or continuation
  consumer, or explicit bounded-incomplete handling costs more than direct
  failure/exception semantics in its real work situations.

## D-054 — Explore Sufficiency Combines Return Adequacy and Continuation Value

- **State**: accepted for design; operational and outcome evidence pending
- **Authority and date**: Sir, 2026-08-08
- **Refines**: the satisfied/bounded-incomplete exit in `D-052`; accepts
  `V-127` as the current operational judgment
- **Decision**:
  - Information is enough only when the supported result is adequate for the
    Frame's purpose, material scope, and residual-consequence tolerance, and no
    feasible authorized next discriminator has material positive net value.
  - Judge the next discriminator qualitatively: identify the material
    uncertainty, the best next observation/move, which plausible outcomes
    would change or secure the return, and whether that improvement justifies
    acquisition, delay, attention, side effect, opportunity cost, and authority.
  - Keep epistemic sufficiency distinct from justified stopping. If the result
    is inadequate but no feasible, authorized, proportionate next move remains,
    return bounded-incomplete under `D-053` rather than claiming sufficiency.
  - Increase counterpressure with downstream loss, irreversibility,
    propagation, observability difficulty, and open-world uncertainty; cheap,
    reversible action that produces discriminating feedback can lower the
    Explore threshold. Human authority retains material value/risk tolerance.
  - Do not use information amount, source count, subjective confidence, recent
    novelty, model stability, or checklist completion as a universal success
    rule. They may be conditional signals or specialized stopping rules.
- **Rationale**: Value-of-information and resource-bounded reasoning connect
  additional search to downstream improvement and cost. Sequential testing,
  information foraging, and observed Human stopping rules show useful
  specializations but also demonstrate why one universal threshold fails.
- **Consequences**:
  - Strategy methods need return-specific adequacy and continuation tests rather
    than one generic “research thoroughly” instruction.
  - The next design compares the mixed one-axis strategy list with a lighter
    epistemic-job/evidence-path composition.
- **Reopen trigger**: The qualitative test causes fabricated value judgments or
  more stopping inconsistency than a simpler return-relative rule, or a common
  measurable threshold works across representative Explore situations.

## D-055 — Explore Uses One Composed Route and Progressive Job Methods

- **State**: accepted for design; method detail and outcome evidence pending
- **Authority and date**: Sir, 2026-08-08
- **Refines**: `D-051`; accepts the recommendation in `V-130..V-131`
- **Decision**:
  - Keep one Route control point. When consequential, compose the next move from
    an epistemic job about a target and a fitting evidence path; do not preserve
    the mixed flat five-strategy list or materialize a full job/access matrix.
  - Use Lookup, Model, Generate, and Discriminate as non-exhaustive epistemic job
    families. They may compose, recur, and change without becoming stages or
    new Working Postures.
  - Keep Lookup compressed by default. Continue case-derived progressive
    Explore method design only for Model, Generate, and Discriminate, which
    currently show distinct failure and sufficiency behavior.
  - Describe evidence paths only as precisely as the move requires. Repository
    tool/context method, Human elicitation, bounded probes/effects, and claim
    verification remain interfaces with their applicable Sub-agent, Human
    collaboration, effect-gate, and Verification owners.
- **Rationale**: Composition preserves the useful orthogonality discovered by
  sufficiency analysis without creating selection ceremony, fake matrix cells,
  or duplicated SOP ownership.
- **Consequences**:
  - `Probe` is no longer a peer Explore strategy; it is one possible
    evidence-production path usable by several jobs and postures.
  - `Model` means the smallest representation adequate for the Frame, never
    open-ended whole-system understanding.
  - The consolidated Explore topology must show job methods as Route branches,
    not a new linear sequence.
- **Reopen trigger**: Real work needs a stable job absent from the four families,
  the composed grammar does not improve method choice, or a specialist method
  demonstrates ownership incompatible with Explore.

## D-056 — Consolidated Explore Topology Is Faithful

- **State**: accepted for design; real-task effect pending
- **Authority and date**: Sir, 2026-08-08
- **Consolidates**: `D-049..D-055`; accepts `V-133`, subject to the posture-tool
  correction in `D-057`
- **Decision**:
  - The current Explore topology is: material information need; compressed
    Lookup when answer/path are obvious, otherwise Frame; one composed Route;
    fitting job method through an evidence interface; supported information
    plus residual; then continue/reframe/reroute/satisfied/bounded-incomplete/
    return-control disposition according to sufficiency.
  - Lookup, Model, Generate, and Discriminate are Route branches that may recur
    and compose, not linear stages.
  - Satisfied and bounded-incomplete are epistemic return dispositions. Using
    another Working Posture method or returning control is orthogonal control,
    not a third completion status.
- **Rationale**: Sir confirmed that the consolidated explanation faithfully and
  clearly represents the accepted components.
- **Consequences**:
  - [`design/48`](../design/48-explore-posture-sop-synthesis.md) becomes the
    current Explore synthesis; earlier dossiers preserve derivation.
  - Specialist method derivation may refine job boundaries without silently
    changing the accepted core.
- **Reopen trigger**: A specialist method or real task reveals a missing common
  control relation or contradiction among the consolidated parts.

## D-057 — Working Postures Are Stateless Methods, Not Work Lifecycles

- **State**: accepted as a common Working Posture design principle
- **Authority and date**: Sir, 2026-08-08
- **Refines**: `D-047`, `D-052`, and `D-056`; replaces lifecycle language while
  preserving use conditions, method, stopping judgment, and returns
- **Decision**:
  - Treat a Working Posture like a pen, paper, or method kept at hand: use it
    whenever it helps, combine it with other methods, and put it down whenever
    it no longer helps. It has no enter/active/switch/exit runtime lifecycle.
  - A posture SOP may state when the method is useful, how to apply it well,
    what correctness/feedback conditions matter, and how to judge continuation
    or return. These are method semantics, not lifecycle transitions or
    mandatory ceremony.
  - Do not require one active posture, posture status reporting, Task Packet
    posture state, transition events, or complete execution of a posture SOP as
    a checklist. Progressive disclosure means consulting/loading useful method
    depth, not activating a mode.
  - Rules that must always hold belong to the universal Working Protocol,
    authority/effect guardrails, Verification contracts, or another applicable
    owner. A posture may still have local correctness conditions for claiming
    its return.
  - Human-facing method narration remains optional and consequential: explain
    the method when it helps Sir predict or redirect work, not to announce
    posture state.
- **Rationale**: Lifecycle framing converts a reusable aid into ceremony,
  encourages false exclusivity and status maintenance, and conflicts with the
  accepted recursive/non-linear nature of real work.
- **Consequences**:
  - Replace `activate`, `active posture`, `switch posture`, and `exit posture`
    language in mutable synthesis with use/compose/return wording.
  - Task/Plan, semantic modules, effects, and Human decisions may have state;
    the posture method itself does not.
- **Reopen trigger**: Agents consistently fail to select or persist a necessary
  method without explicit runtime posture state, and a cheaper stateless
  reminder/routing mechanism cannot solve that failure.

## D-058 — Prefer Foundational Composable Methods Over an Exhaustive Posture Catalog

- **State**: accepted as a Working Posture design principle
- **Authority and date**: Sir, 2026-08-08
- **Extends**: `D-057`; accepts composition as the primary scaling mechanism
- **Decision**:
  - Provide a small basis of atomic/primitive/foundational work methods that an
    Agent can combine, recur, and nest like building blocks; do not enumerate
    every advanced work situation as another posture.
  - “Atomic” means the smallest stable method with a distinct purpose, useful
    application guidance, and return validity—not an indivisible cognitive
    micro-action. Further splitting must reduce total selection/application
    cost rather than merely increase purity.
  - Recurrent higher-order compositions may later earn specialist guidance only
    when the composition itself has a stable method, consumer, failure mode, and
    complexity return. Do not materialize a combinatorial catalog.
- **Rationale**: Composition increases expressive power without expanding the
  Human/Agent vocabulary in proportion to every mixed task shape.
- **Consequences**:
  - Later method derivation must test composability and resist naming advanced
    mixtures prematurely.
  - The current Explore job methods can compose without becoming more Working
    Postures.
- **Reopen trigger**: Agents cannot reliably compose the foundations in a
  recurring high-value situation, and one named higher-order method measurably
  reduces total error and coordination cost.

## D-059 — Minimize the SVC-Specific Common Ground Required for Collaboration

- **State**: accepted as a cross-corpus product/design principle
- **Authority and date**: Sir, 2026-08-08
- **Applies to**: all future SVC corpus/content, Human surfaces, naming, and
  capability design; task-local writing draft updated accordingly
- **Decision**:
  - Minimize the SVC-specific concepts Human and Agent must both learn, recall,
    and align on to collaborate correctly. Prefer plain language and familiar
    concepts; admit a new term only when it removes enough recurring ambiguity,
    control loss, or reasoning cost to repay learning, recall, translation,
    task-switching, and version-drift cost.
  - Human predictability should primarily come from a clear current action,
    method, expected return, authority boundary, and decision—not internal
    mechanism/status vocabulary.
  - Progressive specialist or Agent-oriented depth may exist without becoming
    required Human common ground. Human-facing projections translate only the
    consequential meaning.
  - One admitted concept gets one stable name and local bootstrap. Do not keep
    synonyms, acronyms, or lifecycle/state vocabulary merely because the
    implementation uses them.
- **Rationale**: Every SVC-specific concept consumes scarce Human attention and
  increases coordination setup/recovery cost across concurrent Tasks. A
  framework that improves internal control while making collaboration harder
  defeats `O-INTERACTION`.
- **Consequences**:
  - Concept admission and corpus writing must include a common-ground cost test.
  - Existing names such as Working Posture and SOP remain challengeable rather
    than protected by sunk cost.
- **Reopen trigger**: A specialized vocabulary demonstrably produces better
  Human control and result quality than plain-language projection at lower total
  recurring cost.

## D-060 — Use Working Method, Progressive Guidance, Semantic Bootstrap, and Scoped Guardrails

- **State**: accepted for design; durable corpus landing and real-task effect
  pending
- **Authority and date**: Sir, 2026-08-08
- **Refines**: `D-047`, `D-057..D-059`; accepts the comparison and recommendation
  in [`design/50`](../design/50-working-method-guidance-bootstrap-and-guardrails.md)
- **Decision**:
  - Use **Working Method** for the stateless, composable object and
    progressively disclosed **guidance** for its explanatory content. Retain
    “posture” at most as an informal metaphor and do not create a separate
    `Methodology` framework object.
  - Give a method the smallest semantic bootstrap needed for discovery:
    Purpose, Use when, and Return. These are meanings to communicate, not a
    mandatory schema or lifecycle; one sentence may carry all three.
  - Prefer `Use when` to lifecycle-sounding `Trigger` in method guidance. Use
    the existing term **Guardrail**, with explicit scope, for mandatory
    boundaries rather than adding `Working Law` now.
  - Preserve `D-058`'s atomicity boundary: a foundation is the smallest stable,
    management-useful, behavior-changing method—not a cognitive micro-action.
  - Treat action/method/return as collaboration semantics available when
    consequential, not a continuously monitored Human status surface. Human
    expectation may remain coarse and the exact return may emerge during work;
    expose more when direction, cost, risk, authority, or likely return changes
    materially.
- **Rationale**: Familiar stateless terms preserve the useful method model with
  less compliance, lifecycle, and common-ground baggage. Semantic rather than
  schematic bootstrap supports routing and progressive disclosure without
  turning a tool at hand into ceremony. Sparse, consequential projection
  matches actual Human attention better than continuous method telemetry.
- **Consequences**:
  - Mutable design synthesis should now use Working Method/guidance terminology;
    historical decisions and dossiers may preserve earlier names as derivation.
  - The next inquiry derives the Model specialist method independently before
    comparing it with the cross-method pattern ledger.
- **Reopen trigger**: Generic “method” proves undiscoverable or ambiguous in
  representative work; a small explicit schema improves routing enough to
  repay ceremony; or Human control repeatedly fails without a more continuous
  projection.
