# Decisions D-081..D-090

## D-081 — Human Collaboration Uses Typed Meaning, Semantic Control, and Attention-Value Routing

- **State**: accepted at capability-model depth; exact corpus wording and real-
  interaction effect remain open
- **Authority and date**: Sir, 2026-08-10
- **Accepts**: `V-216..V-220` and
  [`design/70`](../design/70-human-collaboration-guidance.md)
- **Decision**:
  - Optimize consequential result quality and alignment per unit of Human
    attention, interruption, explanation, correction, and rework—not message
    count, autonomy, or approval continuity alone.
  - Recover the intended benefit and type Human meaning before integration.
    Intent/preference, effect authority, material value trade-off, and
    acceptance have their applicable Human authority; factual/causal/technical
    claims and proposed solutions/methods/terms remain fallible inputs.
  - Human wording can be a generative pointer rather than a requested ontology.
    Improve its form while preserving intent; ask only when materially
    different interpretations change scope, authority, value, or acceptance.
  - Proceed autonomously with safe Inquiry, review, reasoning, option/solution
    construction, and Task Packet maintenance. Interact when Human-only
    contribution is necessary or expected early-correction value exceeds
    interruption/task-switching cost.
  - Give the Human one decision-ready consequential return at useful
    resolution: facts versus uncertainty, status quo/real alternatives,
    stakeholder/product/technical/lifecycle consequences, recommendation, and
    exact contribution needed. Use progressive carriers and do not transfer
    raw analysis.
  - Fine control uses relevant semantic handles—outcome, taste, constraint,
    effect, evidence/acceptance horizon, stakeholder consequence, cost,
    urgency, and reversibility—not continuous approval or method telemetry.
  - Existing Design and Test Design create causal left-shift pressure: expose
    Human-owned ambiguity and qualification gaps before expensive commitment.
    This reduces late-interaction risk without adding a mandatory phase,
    complete upfront design, approval checkpoint, or durable `Left-Shift` term.
- **Rationale**: Typed authority prevents both deference and value
  appropriation. Attention-value routing and decision compression allocate
  scarce Human judgment where it changes consequences, while early Design/Test
  Design surfaces reduce the risk that autonomy defers material correction.
- **Reopen trigger**: Typed interpretation causes recurring divergence, Human
  intervention routinely arrives after high-cost rework, or Humans need more
  continuous visibility/control than semantic projections provide.

## D-082 — Specialist Guidance Is Method-Owned; Working Protocol Owns Navigation and Capability Seams

- **State**: accepted at capability-model depth; exact corpus placement and
  real retrieval effect remain open
- **Authority and date**: Sir, 2026-08-10
- **Accepts**: the corrected ownership topology in
  [`design/71`](../design/71-specialist-guidance-routing.md); refines `D-080`
- **Decision**:
  - Specialist guidance is progressive depth selected from within the
    applicable Working Method, not a sibling route directly under Working
    Protocol.
  - Cross-cutting Verification, Human authority, Sub-agent delegation, and
    context/rule services remain independently owned capability interfaces
    invoked by the current method/action.
  - Roles, tools, deterministic transforms, and Assignments are realizations of
    bounded work; their existence and authority do not follow from a guidance
    label.
  - Working Protocol owns compact discoverability and the universal return,
    authority, effect, feedback, integration, and disposition invariants. It
    does not own a catalog-wide `direct versus specialist` runtime router.
  - The earlier six considerations remain possible admission pressure for a
    reusable guidance surface, role, tool, or capability, not a universal
    runtime checklist.
- **Rationale**: Discoverability from one operational entry does not transfer
  semantic ownership. Separating method depth, capability interface, and
  realization preserves progressive disclosure without recreating Working
  Protocol as an umbrella or detaching guidance from the method it improves.
- **Reopen trigger**: Method-local routes repeatedly miss indispensable depth,
  duplicated navigation costs more than a shared index, or an independent
  capability cannot integrate through the accepted universal connections.

## D-083 — Sub-agents Use Two Surfaces, Star Ownership, Self-Loading Context, and Return Validation

- **State**: accepted and refined by `D-085` at capability-model depth;
  delegation economics, role admission, exact interfaces, and validator levels
  remain open
- **Authority and date**: Sir, 2026-08-10
- **Accepts**: the corrected proposition in
  [`design/73`](../design/73-sub-agent-surfaces-and-context-loading.md)
- **Decision**:
  - Model SVC Sub-agents through two coupled surfaces: reusable preset profiles
    and general use guidance. Runtime spawn/wait/cancel behavior is a
    realization, not a third semantic surface.
  - In the default star model, the Primary retains global Task state, Human
    collaboration, unresolved decisions, cross-return integration, and
    residual disposition. A child owns one bounded Assignment and its
    authorized effect surface.
  - The child self-loads applicable reusable SVC/project context progressively
    from canonical sources. The Primary normally supplies Task-specific delta
    plus material handles, not a bundled SVC corpus or full Lead conversation.
  - Profile and Assignment remain distinct: the former carries stable recurring
    specialization; the latter binds one Task-specific invocation.
  - Use **delegated-return validator**, shortened to **validator** within the
    Sub-agent surface, for the mechanism that judges one candidate return
    against its bounded Assignment/integration need.
  - The exact/relational/probabilistic/structured-argument/not-cheaply-
    validatable levels are scoped to delegation economics and result admission;
    they do not define general Verification solutions. `SA` owns this model.
  - A Reviewer Agent is not a validator by role. It may challenge a return or
    execute/orchestrate a complex validation plan, while its non-entailed
    synthesis remains structured argument.
- **Rationale**: This preserves context-isolation value without duplicating
  shared instructions, keeps global authority and integration explicit, and
  gives validation cost its correct place in delegation economics, and prevents
  a role label or correlated LLM judgment from being mistaken for a trusted
  verdict without expanding one local model into general Verification.
- **Reopen trigger**: Children cannot reliably discover canonical context,
  star mediation becomes the dominant bottleneck, nested/decentralized
  ownership is repeatedly necessary, or a real verification architecture
  requires an Agent to occupy a precisely defined trusted validation role.

## D-084 — Sub-agent Causal Levers Require Distinct Economic Judgments

- **State**: accepted at capability-model depth; exact discriminators and
  corpus wording remain open
- **Authority and date**: Sir, 2026-08-11
- **Accepts**: the three causal levers and the need for lever-specific
  economics in [`design/74`](../design/74-sub-agent-causal-benefits-and-admission.md)
- **Decision**:
  - The three causal reasons for Sub-agent delegation are attention partition,
    trajectory shaping, and capacity scaling.
  - They do not share one literal cost formula. Attention partition values
    preserved Primary capacity and avoided context coupling; trajectory shaping
    values a changed result distribution or decision-relevant information;
    capacity scaling values reduced critical-path time.
  - Specialization and diversification remain distinct trajectory-shaping
    returns: one improves a candidate through a local method/feedback loop; the
    other changes candidate coverage and does not thereby validate a decision.
  - Shared Assignment, child-resource, delegated-return validation,
    integration, coordination/conflict, and residual-loss costs remain on the
    comparison side against the best credible direct alternative.
  - When multiple levers coexist, avoid double-counting the same avoided work.
    Preserve a consequence vector and apply Task-specific stakeholder value,
    constraints, reversibility, and cost of delay instead of prescribing a
    universal numeric calculator.
- **Rationale**: The levers change different scarce quantities. Treating them
  as interchangeable cost savings hides the actual counterfactual and can make
  context relief, better decisions, and elapsed time appear commensurable when
  they are not.
- **Reopen trigger**: Representative Tasks reveal another irreducible causal
  benefit, one lever consistently decomposes into independently managed
  economics, or Agents cannot make stable choices without a more concrete
  discriminator.

## D-085 — Authority Star Is Not Candidate/Evidence Transport

- **State**: accepted and refined by `D-087`; its candidate/effect route no
  longer applies to every delegated information result
- **Authority and date**: Sir, 2026-08-11
- **Accepts**:
  [`design/76`](../design/76-sub-agent-transport-and-escalation-reopening.md)
  and refines `D-083`
- **Decision**:
  - Keep the default star as an authority/commitment topology: Primary owns
    global Task/Human/decision/cross-boundary authority; a Child owns bounded
    Assignment work and its authorized effect surface. Do not infer that all
    Child data must first pass through Primary.
  - Retire one unqualified Primary-facing “Return” in Sub-agent guidance.
    Distinguish candidate `Y`, certificate `W`, validator verdict `Q`, and
    decision-sized escalation `E`.
  - A Child normally writes `Y/W` to an assignment-addressable carrier. The
    delegated-return validator consumes that carrier directly; rejection and
    bounded diagnostics return to the Child's local feedback loop; acceptance
    is consumed by an effect gate under the pre-authorized effect budget.
  - Primary receives status at most and consumes `E` only when a validated or
    explicitly unvalidated issue materially changes global route, authority,
    loss/risk, or an unresolved decision. It is not the default relay or
    validator for Child local evidence.
  - Primary direct review remains possible only as an explicit, economical
    admission choice. A broad result requiring Primary semantic synthesis is
    a structured-argument decision-support escalation, not an
    attention-partition return.
  - A Working Method's local result need not be a Primary message. In a Child
    Assignment it normally becomes `Y/W`; the Sub-agent capability owns the
    carrier/validator/effect/escalation route.
- **Rationale**: Routing every candidate and certificate through Primary
  recreates the very context coupling and serial fan-in that delegation is
  supposed to relieve. Separating work evidence from global control preserves
  Primary authority while putting trust in a bounded predicate and effect gate.
- **Reopen trigger**: Candidate carriers create more coordination/drift than
  they save, recurring Tasks need an irreducible Primary evidence review,
  rejection cannot safely re-enter Child local work, or a real effect gate
  proves unable to enforce the declared boundary.

## D-086 — Explorer Is a Candidate Delegated Information-work Contract

- **State**: accepted and refined by `D-087`; Explorer uses a report route by
  default rather than the `D-085` candidate/validator route
- **Authority and date**: Sir, 2026-08-11
- **Accepts**:
  [`design/77`](../design/77-explorer-work-contract-and-guidance-seam.md)
  and refines the Explorer discussion candidate under `D-083..D-085`
- **Decision**:
  - Explorer is not a second Explore Working Method. It is a candidate
    delegated contract for a bounded, consumer-relevant, high-entropy
    information/owner/constraint question whose local retrieval trajectory is
    worth isolating.
  - The Child may compose Explore with Model, Generate, or Discriminate; no
    profile implies a posture lifecycle or one-method-only execution.
  - Its candidate finding-map/provenance follows the `D-085`
    carrier→validator→effect/escalation route. It does not default to a
    Primary-facing report, Task Packet mutation, durable-truth edit, product/
    technical decision, or external effect.
  - Its profile value, if later admitted, must come from stable isolation,
    evidence-carrier, validator, and escalation behavior beyond guidance that
    any Agent can use while directly applying Explore.
- **Rationale**: This preserves the user's intended specialized retrieval
  behavior without creating a one-role-per-method taxonomy or routing local
  evidence through Primary. It gives the profile a falsifiable recurring work
  boundary while retaining direct Explore as the cheaper default.
- **Reopen trigger**: Explorer's boundary collapses into generic Explore
  guidance, its candidate carrier cannot be economically validated/consumed,
  or real Tasks repeatedly require materially different stable contracts.

## D-087 — Delegated Results Follow Their Consumer, Not One Universal Validation Route

- **State**: accepted at capability-model depth; real-task economics remain
  open
- **Authority and date**: Sir, 2026-08-16
- **Accepts**:
  [`design/78`](../design/78-consumer-relative-sub-agent-result-routes.md) and
  refines `D-085..D-086`
- **Decision**:
  - Preserve the authority star, but route a delegated result by what its
    downstream consumer must do with it.
  - An Explorer-like information Assignment normally returns a compact,
    question-shaped report to its semantic consumer. The Primary needs the
    answer, not a verdict that the Child completed work; therefore no generic
    delegated-return validator is inserted.
  - Reading the report and selectively inspecting consequential sources is
    semantic consumption or continued Inquiry, not delegated-return
    validation. This consumption cost remains in the delegation economics.
  - An Executor-like candidate intended to enter a controlled effect surface
    uses the proof-carrying route where applicable: candidate `Y`, support
    `W`, bounded validator verdict `Q`, local repair, effect/integration gate,
    and decision-sized material escalation `E`.
  - `W` may be mechanical proof, traceable evidence, or structured argument;
    these forms must not be called equally strong certificates. Validators
    judge bounded properties, not actor trust or global correctness.
  - No universal Return envelope, certificate ritual, validator stage,
    Reviewer role, or Child-to-Primary data topology follows from Sub-agent use.
- **Rationale**: The earlier single route solved Primary evidence relay for
  effectful candidates but made information delegation incoherent. Consumer-
  relative routing preserves the reason to delegate: compress a retrieval
  trajectory when the answer is the product, and independently qualify a
  bounded candidate when state may change.
- **Reopen trigger**: Information reports repeatedly require the same cheap
  independent predicate before consumption, candidate/effect work cannot use
  direct carriers economically, or the two routes omit a recurring result
  consumer with materially different authority.

## D-088 — The Minimum Sub-agent Surface Uses Explorer and Executor Contracts

- **State**: accepted at capability-model depth; exact source landing and
  real-task economics remain open
- **Authority and date**: Sir, 2026-08-16
- **Accepts**:
  [`design/79`](../design/79-minimal-sub-agent-capability-and-landing.md)
- **Decision**:
  - Treat Sub-agent use as an economic work-placement decision against Primary
    direct work, progressive loading, deterministic mechanisms, and Human-only
    judgment. Attention partition, trajectory shaping, and capacity scaling
    are evaluation dimensions, not role types.
  - Keep Primary authority global and each Assignment independently useful,
    consumer-bounded, effect-bounded, and sized to the Child's real model,
    context, tools, freshness, and recovery ability.
  - Admit only two initial reusable profile contracts: **Explorer** for a
    bounded high-entropy information question and semantic report; **Executor**
    for a bounded realization/feedback loop whose candidate is independently
    qualified before shared effect.
  - Working Methods own problem-solving logic. Profiles add delegation-specific
    isolation, result route, effect, and failure behavior; concrete tool use
    stays in skills or project guidance.
  - Do not admit a default Reviewer, validator Agent, doc writer, rule resolver,
    Task Packet manager, generic QA profile, fixed team pipeline, profile
    directory, Assignment schema, or CLI orchestrator.
  - Rough landing is one compact `src/sections/sub-agents.md` owner with the two
    embedded profiles and a conditional Working Protocol link. Split only
    under proven content/retrieval pressure.
- **Rationale**: These two contracts have distinct recurring consumers and
  result routes while remaining small enough to test. The rejected surfaces
  currently repeat methods, name actors without a trusted mechanism, or add
  coordination without demonstrated net value.
- **Reopen trigger**: Either profile fails to repay its integration/
  qualification cost, collapses into generic Working Method guidance, or a
  different recurring boundary demonstrates a stronger stable return and
  consumer contract.

## D-089 — Verification Is Claim-relative Qualification with Consumer-owned Disposition

- **State**: accepted at capability-model depth; exact source landing and
  real-task qualification behavior remain open
- **Authority and date**: Sir, 2026-08-16
- **Accepts**:
  [`design/80`](../design/80-minimal-verification-capability-and-landing.md)
- **Decision**:
  - Verification follows `owned claim → discriminating observation/oracle →
    evidence + trusted-base scope + residual → consumer disposition`.
  - Product/Technical Design owns expected claims; Test Design owns meaningful
    challenge/oracle design; Implementation builds mechanisms; Verification
    executes/interprets them; the consuming authority/effect gate owns
    acceptance, waiver, integration, publication, or other consequence.
  - Prefer the smallest credible mechanism for the loss at stake. Determinism
    is not validity, and no fixed test pyramid/ladder is universal.
  - Reuse a qualified module guarantee by checking consumer connection and
    assumptions; retest composition only where it creates new behavior, and
    requalify when claims, assumptions, environment, oracle, or connection
    change.
  - Treat same-context AI-generated implementation/fixture/oracle/test as
    correlated candidate evidence; strengthen it only when the expected loss
    repays independent or relational mechanisms.
  - Verification remains distributed. A Task-root `verification.md` is
    pressure-created cross-return synthesis, not a final phase. Rough landing
    adds one compact `src/sections/verification.md`; no universal Reviewer,
    assurance schema, mandatory acceptance file, or CLI engine is added.
- **Rationale**: This preserves product/technical meaning, proof economy, and
  authority while preventing green tests, deterministic tools, or another
  Agent from silently becoming acceptance.
- **Reopen trigger**: Real tasks cannot route non-deterministic/Human evidence,
  local evidence is repeatedly lost across returns, modular reuse hides
  composition failures, or the capability's reading/state cost exceeds the
  proof failures it prevents.

## D-090 — Taste Is Progressive, Use-case-routed Design Judgment

- **State**: accepted at capability-model depth; exact source landing and
  real-task retrieval/effect remain open
- **Authority and date**: Sir, 2026-08-16
- **Accepts**:
  [`design/81`](../design/81-minimal-taste-design-capability-and-landing.md)
- **Decision**:
  - Distinguish project Product/Technical truth, Sir's legitimate personal
    preference, rebuttable general judgment, and Task-local hypotheses without
    imposing a fixed metadata schema.
  - Keep taste as method-owned progressive Design depth, retrieved through the
    Product/Technical/Test solution projection and a recurring local design
    pressure—not as a global maxim pile, phase, or required document.
  - Useful taste connects a quality/consequence, use pressure, preferred
    default and causal reason, cost/counter-pressure, weakening conditions,
    and observable example or challenge when useful.
  - Match the review carrier to the judgment: visual/replay/Human perception
    for UI/UX, topology/change/lifecycle consequences for architecture, and
    code/contracts/tests/observability for implementation.
  - Consume ECCA as rebuttable semantic-locality, ownership/contract, vertical-
    coherence, and propagation guidance—not as a universal branded architecture
    or directory prescription.
  - Rough landing adds one compact Design/taste router and reuses
    `src/sections/implementation-taste.md`; deeper UI/UX or Architecture owners
    are created only after recurring content/retrieval pressure proves value.
- **Rationale**: Use-case routing gives Design judgment a consumer and causal
  role while preserving Sir's opinionated experience, Agent challenge, and
  progressive disclosure. It avoids both sterile universalism and an inert
  shelf of wise-sounding rules.
- **Reopen trigger**: Guidance is not retrieved when needed, personal taste is
  confused with project/universal truth, the three projections fragment one
  solution, or a domain repeatedly requires distinct deeper ownership.
