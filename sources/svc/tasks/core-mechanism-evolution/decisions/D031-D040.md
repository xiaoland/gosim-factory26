# Decisions D-031..D-040

## D-031 — Discussion/Design Exploration Proceeds Without Approval Stops

- **State**: accepted
- **Authority and date**: Sir, 2026-08-05
- **Decision**: During the current discussion/design stage, the Lead continues
  exploration, derivation, evidence collection, thinking, and task-packet
  maintenance without requesting approval. It pauses only when Sir must provide
  missing information, review a sufficiently mature proposal, or decide a
  material direction/trade-off.
- **Rationale**: Approval stops on reversible analytical work waste Human
  attention and interrupt synthesis; Human involvement is most valuable at real
  information, review, and authority boundaries.
- **Consequences**:
  - Commentary updates do not become disguised approval requests.
  - Task-packet maintenance remains continuous and autonomous.
  - This does not authorize durable SVC source, CLI/runtime, external, commit,
    or other mutation outside the existing gate.
- **Reopen trigger**: Autonomous exploration repeatedly diverges from the shared
  problem model or produces review packets too large for efficient correction.

## D-032 — Cell Parallelism Defaults to One Plan with a Bounded Multi-Plan Exception

- **State**: accepted
- **Authority and date**: Sir, 2026-08-05
- **Refines**: `D-029`; accepts `V-059`
- **Decision**: A Cell owns one current partial linear Plan by default. Parallel
  executors that produce one aggregate return remain Assignments under one
  Slice with an explicit join/verifier. Distinct continuing obligations or exit
  horizons correct the Track/Phase topology. Multiple current Plans inside one
  Cell are allowed only when their returns are independently integratable,
  still share one truthful Cell, materially benefit from concurrency, and have
  an explicit integration/conflict rule.
- **Rationale**: Agent or tool concurrency does not itself create planning
  topology. The default preserves a legible linear route, while the bounded
  exception avoids serializing valuable independent returns that cannot be
  represented more truthfully on another Task axis.
- **Consequences**:
  - A joined return has one Slice owner even when several Agents produce its
    evidence or candidate parts.
  - A Cell with multiple Plans owns only their fronts, material relations, and
    integration rule; it does not create a super-Plan or turn any Plan into a
    DAG.
  - Plan completion returns state to the Cell; only the Cell's semantic exit
    predicate can satisfy its Phase contribution.
- **Reopen trigger**: Real Cells repeatedly force artificial Tracks or harmful
  serialization, or multi-Plan Cells make return effects and integration less
  predictable than the concurrency benefit warrants.

## D-033 — Phase Reopening and Active Overlap Use Bounded Exact Semantics

- **State**: accepted
- **Authority and date**: Sir, 2026-08-06
- **Refines**: `D-028..D-030`; accepts `V-061`
- **Decision**: Phase scope declarations may overlap, but one active Phase Cell
  per Track is the default. A failed attempt or invalid candidate certificate
  before exit replans the affected Cell inside the still-active Phase. Reopen a
  Phase only when new evidence directly defeats the same previously exited
  proposition on its claimed baseline/proof horizon; preserve the former exit
  and identify affected consumers. A materially changed obligation, scope,
  predicate, baseline, or authority boundary becomes new Plan work or a new
  Phase. Simultaneous active Phase Cells on one Track are a bounded exception
  for independently real barriers with positive concurrency value and single
  ownership of every work return.
- **Rationale**: Existing packets use `reopened` for failed acceptance,
  mismatched evidence, and later requirements, even though those events have
  different consequences. The bounded distinction preserves history and
  downstream impact without making every attempt a Phase transition or
  serializing every independent barrier.
- **Consequences**:
  - A downstream Phase may be declared while its shared Track Cell remains
    pending; declared scope overlap is not active overlap.
  - Failure/correction normally remains in the current Cell Plan. Reclassify a
    Gate, refine a Track, or order dependencies before admitting active overlap.
  - Shared work has one Plan owner; other Cells consume its return rather than
    duplicate the work or evidence.
- **Reopen trigger**: Real tasks cannot reliably distinguish exact claim
  invalidation from new obligation, or the single-active default repeatedly
  creates more artificial Track splits or harmful serialization than legibility.

## D-034 — Task-Packet Modules Use Four Object Kinds and Stable Progressive Entries

- **State**: accepted
- **Authority and date**: Sir, 2026-08-06
- **Refines**: `D-014`, `D-018`; accepts the semantic/growth portion of former
  `V-063`
- **Decision**: Distinguish Task packet, semantic module, supporting artifact,
  and nested Task. A concern remains inline until it earns a stable module
  entry; the default growth is inline -> `<module>.md` -> stable
  `<module>.md` plus sibling `<module>/` depth. A nested `packet.md` is reserved
  for an independent Task with its own objective, authority/guardrails,
  verification, resume point, and terminal integration return.
- **Rationale**: A directory, posture, work coordinate, or Agent identity does
  not establish semantic ownership. Stable progressive entries preserve
  discoverability without pre-creating empty structure or moving links when a
  concern gains depth.
- **Consequences**:
  - The module entry owns current integrated state and return path, not merely
    an index or history.
  - Supporting artifacts do not acquire independent task state by being split.
  - Exact concern-module names, content contracts, and templates remain to be
    derived; the former work-topology directories were not accepted.
- **Reopen trigger**: The sibling file/directory convention repeatedly confuses
  consumers or requires aliases/moves, or independent work cannot be recovered
  without nested packets under the accepted boundary.

## D-035 — Mechanical Sharding Value Is Independent of Semantic Module Admission

- **State**: accepted
- **Authority and date**: Sir, 2026-08-06
- **Refines**: `D-022`, `D-034`
- **Decision**: Deterministic physical sharding—such as fixed decision-ID
  ranges—may have positive management value by bounding Agent reading, editing,
  diff, and conflict cost. That value is evaluated separately from whether the
  shards are semantic task-packet modules.
- **Rationale**: One logical decision owner can become expensive to manipulate
  as a single thousand-line file. Storage partition can reduce that cost
  without inventing independent meaning, authority, lifecycle, or roll-up for
  each shard.
- **Consequences**:
  - A module entry/index continues to own routing and integrated current state.
  - A stable key-to-shard rule may be mechanical rather than subject-based.
  - Exact admission thresholds and shard rules remain module-specific; equal
    narrative chunks are not accepted by analogy.
- **Reopen trigger**: Sharding routinely increases navigation/synchronization
  cost or creates de facto competing state owners that outweigh bounded file
  operations.

## D-036 — Packet Shape Stabilizes at Topology Admission with a Human Map Projection

- **State**: accepted
- **Authority and date**: Sir, 2026-08-06
- **Refines**: `D-018`, `D-027`, `D-029`, `D-034`; accepts `V-067` and
  `V-068`
- **Decision**: When work topology activates, `packet.md` keeps one compact
  Human-language table/list projection of current Plan-owner/Cell state,
  current return/next action, and material Human attention. Packet shape is
  stabilized when credible semantic topology is admitted rather than after
  files become long. Every real participating Track × Phase Cell immediately
  receives `cells/<track>-<phase>.md` as its stable entry and default Plan
  owner, even when the Plan currently ends TBC; coordinates outside declared
  Phase scope are not created. One-axis Plan owners receive stable owner entries
  under the same trigger without default peer collection directories.
- **Rationale**: Depth-reactive splitting relies on an LLM later remembering to
  reorganize accumulated state, increases irrelevant reads/shared-file edits,
  and causes late path/ownership migration. Once topology is real, its Plan
  owners already have enough management meaning to justify stable addresses.
- **Consequences**:
  - Cell entry owns Plan truth, `task-map.md` owns integrated routing/barriers,
    and `packet.md` owns the consequential Human projection.
  - Topology admission is incomplete until required owner entries and the Human
    projection exist; shape growth is not optional cleanup.
  - A task-packet-growth Agent/reminder may later audit drift but is not the
    primary mechanism; mechanically checkable invariants may be considered
    only after semantics stabilize.
- **Reopen trigger**: Early topology/path corrections and roll-up synchronization
  cost more than the targeted retrieval, edit isolation, delegation, and Human
  switching benefits they provide.

## D-037 — Inquiry and Diagnosis Share an Epistemic Module Family

- **State**: accepted
- **Authority and date**: Sir, 2026-08-06
- **Refines**: `D-014..D-016`, `D-034`, `D-036`; accepts `V-070`
- **Decision**: Inquiry and Diagnosis are one epistemic task-packet module
  family with stable `inquiry.md` and `diagnosis.md` variants selected by the
  owned uncertainty. Activate the entry early under material multi-source,
  method/sampling, causal-alternative, provenance, cross-consumer, delegation,
  or Human reasoning-review pressure; keep bounded lookups with their consuming
  owner. The entry owns current synthesis, evidence boundary, and next
  discriminator, while same-stem depth holds semantic cases/probes/returns.
  The applicable Task/Track/Phase/Cell owner retains the Plan, and only a
  Lead-integrated inquiry return changes task state.
- **Rationale**: Inquiry and Diagnosis share evidence discipline and return
  semantics but differ in whether they begin from an open question or observed
  mismatch. Treating them as postures/files or separate Plans either hides
  stable epistemic ownership or duplicates work sequencing.
- **Consequences**:
  - Facts, interpretations, supported conclusions, and generalizations remain
    distinct in proportion to false-accept loss.
  - The module entry is normally the synthesis; no default parallel
    `synthesis.md` or query transcript is created.
  - Inquiry evidence and verification acceptance remain separate while sharing
    observations by reference.
- **Reopen trigger**: The variants repeatedly need incompatible entry/return
  contracts, or early module activation adds more ceremony than search,
  recovery, delegation, and review cost it removes.

## D-038 — Inquiry Carries Material Freshness Without Owning Its Oracle

- **State**: accepted
- **Authority and date**: Sir, 2026-08-06
- **Refines**: `D-037`
- **Decision**: Inquiry/Diagnosis material must expose source/baseline/
  environment freshness, invalidation conditions, stale/unknown disposition,
  and recheck need when these affect consumption. The task packet records and
  routes this validity information but does not invent a universal TTL or
  determine domain freshness independently of the source owner and suitable
  verification surface.
- **Rationale**: A well-supported but stale synthesis can misdirect design and
  implementation. Freshness semantics vary by source, dependency, environment,
  and claim, so centralizing them in task-packet timestamps would create false
  confidence.
- **Consequences**:
  - Stale/unknown material cannot remain presented as unqualified current truth.
  - Evidence time alone is neither freshness nor relevance proof.
  - Exact freshness mechanisms belong to later source/verification/protocol
    design, not this file package contract.
- **Reopen trigger**: Task-local freshness projection cannot prevent stale
  consumption without a stronger shared mechanism, or recording it routinely
  costs more than the affected evidence risk warrants.

## D-039 — Slice Scope Follows Its Return, Not Its Working Posture

- **State**: accepted
- **Authority and date**: Sir, 2026-08-06
- **Refines**: `D-016`, `D-026`, `D-032`; accepts `V-073`
- **Decision**: A material Slice is classified by its primary independently
  useful return, not by the working postures used to produce it. Multi-scope or
  Human-effect-ambiguous Plans use `IQ`, `DS`, `IM`, `VR`, and `RT` scope tags
  with a semantic title and plain effect/authority boundary; simple unambiguous
  one-Slice Tasks need not. One Plan owns one Slice index sequence, and the
  handle is written index-first—`01-IQ -> 02-DS -> 03-IQ`—so tags never create
  separate counters or imply separate Plans. Durable mutation crosses into an
  applicable authorized `IM` Slice; the tag itself grants no authority.
- **Rationale**: Human needs to predict what a Slice will hand back and whether
  it changes the system. Postures recurse within one management unit, while an
  `IQ-03`-style handle visually suggests a tag-specific namespace rather than
  the third Slice of one mixed Plan.
- **Consequences**:
  - Plan and Slice remain the sequencing/management objects; scope tags do not
    create posture-specific Plan files, agents, modules, or directories.
  - A scope transition creates a new Slice only at a real return, authority, or
    integration boundary, not for every internal posture change.
  - Human-Agent updates use scope-appropriate verbs and expand the tag in local
    language rather than relying on unexplained abbreviations.
- **Reopen trigger**: Real Plans show that the five tags or index-first handles
  add more interpretation and maintenance cost than effect predictability, or
  mixed-return work is repeatedly split without independent management value.

## D-040 — Design and Decision Are Separate Task-Packet Modules

- **State**: accepted
- **Authority and date**: Sir, 2026-08-06
- **Refines**: `D-034`, `D-039`; accepts `V-075`
- **Decision**: Design and Decision are adjacent but independently activated
  semantic modules. `design.md` owns the mutable current design synthesis;
  `decisions.md` owns explicit task-local dispositions with authority, scope,
  rationale, consequence, and reopen/supersession semantics. The applicable
  `DS` Slice retains sequencing and return ownership, while `packet.md`
  projects the actual decision-ready Human brief rather than only a link.
- **Rationale**: Design evolves while alternatives and system consequences are
  being reconciled. A decision has a different lifecycle: later work must know
  exactly what was disposed, by whom, and under what invalidation boundary.
  Combining the two makes either the current model hard to revise or accepted
  history easy to rewrite accidentally.
- **Consequences**:
  - Either module may remain inline or activate without the other under its own
    pressure; their stable entries and same-stem depth remain progressive.
  - Supporting design depth follows semantic concerns; decision depth may use
    mechanical ID-range sharding without becoming several semantic modules.
  - Neither module is durable product or technical truth; accepted system
    deltas move through later Plan work to their normal semantic owner.
- **Reopen trigger**: Real Tasks show that independent entries routinely
  duplicate context or drift, and one combined entry preserves both mutable
  synthesis and authoritative disposition at materially lower total cost.
