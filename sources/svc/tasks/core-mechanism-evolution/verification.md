# Verification and Evidence Ledger

## Role

This file owns the mapping from material claims and candidate mechanisms to
their evidence horizon, counterexample, experiment, and result. It does not own
work progress or decisions.

The vocabulary is task-local until experiments justify a durable SVC contract:

- **Interaction**: a Human decision, correction, acceptance, or handoff is
  explicit; no implementation outcome follows.
- **Observed execution**: an action occurred; its relevant result is not proven.
- **Local**: a relevant local check or inspection is observable.
- **Integration/client**: an interacting unit or Consumer boundary is observed.
- **Deployed/external**: the relevant runtime or external boundary is observed.
- **Reported-only**: a participant reports a result without matching evidence.
- **Blocked**: a known precondition prevents the required observation.
- **Unknown**: evidence is absent, partial, stale, contradictory, or ends before
  the relevant outcome.

Work may be closed at one declared horizon while a higher horizon remains
unknown. The distinction must be explicit.

Claim maturity is a separate axis:

- **accepted-input**: Human-authoritative scope or preference for this task
- **supported**: available evidence supports the bounded wording
- **hypothesis**: useful candidate requiring a named test or counterexample
- **refuted**: the stated claim failed its declared test
- **inconclusive**: the attempted proof cannot resolve the claim
- **superseded**: a successor claim replaces this wording without erasing it

Maturity does not imply a particular evidence horizon. A supported local claim
may still have no deployed or external proof.

| Human-Agent interaction | Accepted decision/output quality; intent and product/technical taste alignment; useful control resolution | Human attention, interruption, explanation, review, correction, decision latency, and rework | Authority/scope visibility; option and evidence quality; takeover or redirect behavior |
| Long task trajectory | Terminal task quality and completeness under ambiguity, complexity, interleaving, and changed facts | Agent work, elapsed time, repeated discovery, false starts, integration, recovery, Human supervision, and unresolved risk | State/resumption accuracy; dependency and unknown tracking; replanning; evidence honesty; delegation return |
| Large-system lifecycle | Ability to develop and evolve the system while preserving product and technical quality | Total change cost, impact-discovery cost, coordination, verification, migration/rollout/recovery, regressions, and future change amplification | Owner/contract traceability; dependency impact; coherence; compatibility and proof horizons |
| Simple counterexample | Existing bounded work remains effective | Added reading, fields, files, decisions, time, maintenance, and failure surface | Whether pressure-triggered mechanisms remain inactive |

No metric, numeric threshold, or product-defining subset is accepted yet.
Useful evaluation must compare actual output quality and total cost, not only a
completion marker, process-state accuracy, or apparent Agent throughput.

## Contrasting Rehearsals

| ID | Task shape | Packet pressure | Required observation | Status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `R-SIMPLE` | Bounded read-only diagnosis or local mechanical fix | Any candidate mechanism must normally stay inactive | Compare output quality and total added interaction, reading, state, time, maintenance, and failure surface against the current minimum | not designed |
| `R-LONG` | Ambiguous multi-slice task with many interdependent concerns, changed evidence, false starts, pause/reopen, delegation and handoff | Full problem-solving trajectory, not only packet recovery | Compare terminal result quality/completeness and total Human/Agent cost; diagnose framing, reasoning, state, replanning, integration, recovery, and evidence failures | not designed |
| `R-SYSTEM` | Cross-unit or multi-repository change across contract, implementation, migration, rollout, recovery and later evolution | End-to-end system change economics and lifecycle quality | Compare understanding, impact, change, verification and future-change cost without copying durable truth or inventing enterprise PM state | not designed |

These are task shapes, not fixed benchmark repositories. Fixtures, procedure,
comparison method, and acceptance remain open until the shared outcome model
and representative Consumer episodes are adequate.

## Proof Rules

- A completion marker, Agent report, task state, test command, and external
  observation are not interchangeable.
- Every accepted high-impact mechanism requires a simple-task counterexample
  and a pressure case where its absence is observable.
- Missing, partial, contradictory, stale, or unavailable evidence remains
  explicit; it never defaults to success.
- Historical case evidence can motivate an experiment but cannot by itself
  impose required Consumer behavior.
- A mechanism is admitted only when it has a distinct owner, trigger, consumer,
  state, authority boundary, failure behavior, verification path, and marginal
  complexity return.
- Experiments must count Human maintenance and lead-agent synthesis cost, not
  only apparent Agent throughput.
