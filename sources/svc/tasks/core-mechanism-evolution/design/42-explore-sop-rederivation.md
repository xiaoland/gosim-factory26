## What Went Wrong

The Lead started with a plausible generic information lifecycle, then filled
its six slots with every adjacent gleaning. That produced a neat-looking SOP
without establishing that the steps were specific to Explore:

- rules loading, semantic-owner resolution, and Task Packet current truth are
  general orientation/state concerns
- freshness, claim testing, and independent evidence belong to general
  epistemic/Verification discipline unless Explore needs a distinct form
- state update, integration, and return belong to the universal Working
  Protocol
- forgetting and ablation are conditional epistemic/adaptation methods
- tool routing, noise filtering, meta-planning, and context isolation were
  supplied primarily as an **Explorer sub-agent** method, not as proof of the
  Explore posture's universal SOP

These concerns may be invoked while exploring. Co-occurrence does not make them
posture steps.

## Preserved Step 1 — Frame

For non-trivial Explore, begin with a lightweight, revisable frame:

1. what purpose or useful return the information should support
2. what is not yet understood enough
3. when currently imaginable, what kind of finding could materially change
   the direction

When the domain is too unfamiliar for item 3, first discover enough vocabulary,
boundaries, and candidate information sources to formulate a useful question.
This is a cognitive move, not a mandatory form; obvious lookup remains direct.

## Re-derivation Method

Do not assume that the rest of Explore is a linear sequence. After Frame, first
identify recurring Explore situations and the specific failure each SOP rule
would prevent. Candidate shapes may be a loop, branching strategy selection,
or a small repertoire rather than Steps 2–6.

For each candidate behavior, ask:

1. Is it distinctive to finding key information, or merely universal good
   work/evidence discipline?
2. Does it change what an Agent actually does after Frame?
3. Does it recur across meaningfully different Explore tasks?
4. Is it still useful without an Explorer sub-agent or named tool?
5. Does the guidance prevent a concrete failure at lower cost than ordinary
   Agent judgment?

The next Inquiry should compare real Explore situations—known-question lookup,
unfamiliar-system sensemaking, hypothesis discrimination, alternative search,
and empirical probing—without presuming that these become posture steps or
sub-postures.
