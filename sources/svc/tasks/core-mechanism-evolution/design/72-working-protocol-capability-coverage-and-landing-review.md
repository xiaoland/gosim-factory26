## Failure and Reopen Conditions

Reopen `WP × P1` if a later Track shows that:

- the four universal connections cannot express a necessary delegation,
  qualification, taste, or Task Packet interaction
- method-local progressive routes repeatedly miss indispensable capability
  depth
- the Human needs materially different control than typed authority,
  attention-value interaction, and consequential projections provide
- simple tasks pay recurring protocol ceremony or context cost
- one compact source owner demonstrably outperforms the accepted ownership
  split after retrieval and maintenance cost are counted

Exact corpus landing, source verification, and real-task evaluation remain
future work. They do not keep the capability-model Cell active.

## Disposition

Mark `WP × P1` **satisfied, reopenable**. Foreground `SA × P1` next in
the accepted discussion order. Its first return should determine when context

execution, verification, Lead integration, false-accept, and rework cost,
